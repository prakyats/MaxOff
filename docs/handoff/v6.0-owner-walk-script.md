# MaxOff v6.0 "Admin calendar": the Owner's phone walk (about 20 minutes)

Use the **installed app on your phone**, on the **preview** of this release (`https://phase-v60-maxoff-staging.pixoraclips.workers.dev`, release candidate ‹sha at READY›), never on the live app. Use only the staging test sign-ins, never a real account. Do it at the phone's two sizes if you can (a small phone, 375, and a large one, 430). Steps marked *laptop* are on a laptop browser.
You need three sign-ins: you (Owner), the test Admin, the test Crew member.

Before you start, have ready on one weekday next week (call it **day X**), using today's app on the preview:
- as the **test Admin**: a **Shoot / Site Visit** for the test Crew member on day X, **2:00–4:00 pm**, with a place;
- as **you**: a **Meeting** for another person on day X with **no time** (date only);
- one person on **approved leave** on day X (a full day) and one on an **approved half day**;
- **day Y**, another weekday, with nobody booked and nobody away.

Each step: **do this → you should see this.**

## As Owner (about 8 minutes)

### The month's day
1. **Tap Calendar, then day X** (grow the calendar to the full month and tap the day) → a sheet with the day's events and tasks, then **Busy · 2**: "‹Crew› · Shoot / Site Visit · 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · booked by ‹Admin›" and "‹person› · Meeting · All day · booked by you"; then **On leave** ("‹name›", "‹name› · Half day"); then **Free** with everyone else. No line says "Everyone is available".
2. **Look at the day's chips** → the approved leave is **not** also a chip in the day (On leave says it once). A holiday or weekly off would still have its chip.
3. **At the foot of the sheet** → "+ New task on ‹day X›" pinned there.
4. **Press back** → the sheet closes, you stay on the calendar. **Press back again** → you leave the calendar.
5. **Open day Y** → "Everyone is available", and no Busy, On leave or Free lists.

### Day and Week on a phone
6. **Shrink the calendar to the week strip and tap day X** → under the strip, rows: "Tasks · n", "Client work · n" (if any), a **"Crew busy · 2"** card, "On leave · names", "Free · names", and **"+ New task on ‹day X›" pinned above the bottom bar**. No hour grid.
7. **Tap another day in the strip, then back** → back leaves the calendar (choosing a day adds no step).

### Day and Week on a laptop
8. *(laptop)* **Calendar → Day, on day X** → seven day buttons along the top ("Mon 9" style, day X solid). On the left **Events** (time · kind · title · place, then the client and the people), "Due · n", "Client work · n". On the right a **"Crew · ‹day›"** card: Busy, On leave, Free, then "+ New task on ‹day X›" (outlined).
9. *(laptop)* **Click another day button** → that day shows; the browser's back does not step through the days.
10. *(laptop)* **Calendar → Week** → a table, one row a day: the events and "· n due", **Crew busy** (a line per booking, or "Nobody busy"), **On leave**, **Free** ("Everyone", two names, or "n people"). Today's row is tinted. **Click day X's row** → its Day opens.

### New task, type first
11. **Tap Tasks → New task** → only **Type** to choose and a greyed Title; **Create task** is greyed.
12. **Open Type** → every type with its colour dot and what it asks ("a deadline", "date, time, place · on the calendar"), and under the list "Types come from Settings → Task types…".
13. **Choose Shoot / Site Visit** → When, From, To, Where, Client (label), Who's on it?, and **"Done by … · the end of the event"** with Change, then More options.
14. **Set When to day X, From 2:30 pm, To 4:30 pm** → "Done by ‹day X›, 4:30 pm · the end of the event". **Change To to 5:00 pm** → Done by follows to 5:00 pm.
15. **Tap Add a person** → a sheet "Who's on it?" with "Shoot / Site Visit · ‹day X› · 2:30 pm – 5:00 pm", a search, then **Busy then**: the test Crew member "Shoot / Site Visit 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · booked by ‹Admin›" with an amber **Clashes**; the person with the date-only Meeting "Meeting all day · booked by you" with **Clashes**; the person on leave "On leave that day" with an amber **On leave**. Then **Free then**, with the half day reading "Half day that day". No task title, client or place of anyone else's task anywhere.
16. **Tick the test Crew member and one free person, then tap Done** → the sheet closes (Done only closes it; nothing is saved yet). The form shows two chips ("Clashes", "Free") and a **Primary owner** list.
17. **Tap Change beside Done by and set a different time** → the deadline is yours now: changing To no longer moves it.
18. **Press back** → the form asks to discard. **Discard.** Press back again → you leave Tasks.
19. **New task again, choose Normal** → one Deadline and Time, Client (label), Who's on it?, More options. Pick a day and tap **Add a person** → one list, no groups, each person's day ("1 task already due", "Nothing that day", "On leave that day").
20. **Open More options** → Start from (a template), Priority, Reminders, Checked first by, Description, Stages. Create the task.
21. **Open that task → ⋯ → Edit task** → the same form, the type already chosen, the people as chips. Close it with back.

### From the calendar
22. **Calendar → day X → "+ New task on ‹day X›"** → the form on day X (the event's When for an event type, the deadline for any other). Close it with back; back again closes the day.

## As the test Admin (about 7 minutes)

23. **Sign in as the Admin. Calendar → day X** → Busy lists **your own booking too**: "‹Crew› · Shoot / Site Visit · 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · **booked by you**", and the Owner's "… · Meeting · All day · **booked by Owner**". This day used to read "Everyone is available" for an Admin who had booked people: it must not now.
24. **The same day** → the Owner's meeting shows only its kind, time and "booked by Owner": no title, no client, no place. On leave says "On leave" or "· Half day", never the kind of leave. You (the Owner) are never under Free or On leave.
25. **Your own shoot** → it shows in the day's list ("Your tasks" on the phone, **"Your events"** on the laptop's Day) **and** in Busy.
26. **Day Y** → "Everyone is available".
27. *(laptop)* **Day and Week on day X** → as in steps 8 and 10, with "Your events" on the left.
28. **Tasks → New task → Shoot / Site Visit on day X, 3:00–5:00 pm → Add a person** → Busy then: the test Crew member "… booked by you" with Clashes. Back closes the sheet; back again asks to discard the form.
29. **Edit your shoot for the Crew member** → the same form. The Crew member is **not** shown as clashing with this very shoot (a task does not clash with itself). Close with back.
30. **People** → someone with a date-only event today shows **Busy** all day.

## The test Crew member (about 1 minute)

31. **Sign in as Crew. Open Calendar on day X** → as before v6.0: your own shoot on the hour grid, no Busy, On leave or Free lists, nobody else's bookings. "Suggest a task" instead of New task.

## The guide (about 3 minutes)

Open **Me → How MaxOff works** and read:
32. **Owner:** "Create a task" (type first, Done by and Change, Who's on it? with its picture), "Use the calendar" (the day's Busy / On leave / Free, Day and Week in words), "Open a day and add a task to it".
33. **Admin:** "Create a task" (its picture shows the list beside the form with one person under Busy then), "Use the calendar" (its picture shows a Busy row), "A day and a task from it".
34. **Crew:** the calendar topic is unchanged.

Each topic should say what you just saw. Each sheet in the guide closes with back.

## Tidy up
35. As Owner or the Admin, cancel the test tasks made for the walk (with a reason) and the test leave, if you want the preview clean.

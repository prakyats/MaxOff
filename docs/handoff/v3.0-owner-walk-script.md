# v3.0 "Reports": the owner's phone walk

About 10 minutes, on the phone with the installed app, on the **phase preview** (staging data), never production. One Owner sign-in and one Admin sign-in.

## As the Owner
1. **More → Reports.** Under the month strip the list has five rows: Month, End of day, **People**, **Stage durations** and **Client work**. Each new row has a one-line description.
2. **Tap People.** The screen opens on **This month**: Week · *Month* · Custom, with the arrow back to last month.
   - Under "Tasks by person", **Employees** come first, one card each, **in name order**. Nothing is coloured or ranked.
   - Each card shows Completed, On time, Revision loops, To first hand-in, Acknowledgement lag, Open now and Overdue now.
   - Under a line, the same card shows Days worked, Additional leave, Comp leave, Late days, Left early (not answered), Early leaves approved and Half days.
   - Freelancers have their own block. People with no tasks are named in one line under the cards.
3. **Tap Week.** The attendance part of each card goes away (it is shown in Month only). **Tap the left arrow:** last week.
4. **Back gesture once:** you are on Reports, not on an earlier week. Back again leaves Reports.
5. **People → scroll to "Late and overdue tasks".**
   - Three small cards count the late tasks by person (in name order), by type and by client label.
   - Below them is the list: title, the primary owner, "Due 5 Nov · handed in 2 days late" or "still open, 3 days past", and the late reason.
   - **Tap one:** the task opens. Back returns to People.
6. **Tap Custom.** Pick From and To dates and tap **Show**. The cards change to that range. **Re-check (walk fix):** pick 1 Jan 2024 – today. The report shows the **latest** year, ending today, and a line under the dates says so: "Showing 26 Nov 2025 – 26 Nov 2026 (up to a year)". The figures are not zero.
7. **Reports → Stage durations.**
   - "Every project" lists each stage name once, even when projects spell it differently ("Edit" and "edit" count together), slowest first: "Median 3 days · longest 8 days · 12 done". The name shown is its most common spelling, so "Ideation", never "ideation", when both are used (walk fix).
   - "By project" shows the same for each project.
8. **Reports → Client work.**
   - "Totals" (All clients) comes first, then one card per client: Planned in the period (done of planned), On time, Done in the period, Overdue now, On hold now and Projects completed.
   - **No ₹ amounts anywhere.** Revenue joins this screen with v2.1.
   - **Re-check (walk fix):** scroll down, then tap a client's name. The client opens **at its top**, with its header showing. Back returns to the report.
9. **Speed:** each report shows its header and Week · Month · Custom at once, with the period you chose already highlighted and its line ("This month") in place (walk fix). The cards fill in a moment later, without anything jumping.
10. **Help → search "People report"**: the guide topic matches the screen. Do the same for "Stage durations" and "Client work".

## As an Admin
11. **More → Reports.** It is still your own work report, with the four cards and Deliverables. You see no People, Stage durations or Client work rows.
12. In "Where deliverables wait longest", stage names that differ only in capitals are one line. A stage in a named group shows the group in brackets: "4 waiting at Collect info (Edit stage) · median 26 days" (walk fix).
13. **Re-check (walk fix):** typing `/reports/people` in the address shows No access with "This report is for the Owner", not "Reports are for the Owner".

Nothing. They have no Reports.

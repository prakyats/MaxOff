# MaxOff v6.5 "Team view": the Owner's phone walk (about 15 minutes)

Use the **installed app on your phone**, on the **preview** of this release (`https://phase-v65-maxoff-staging.pixoraclips.workers.dev`, release candidate ‹sha at READY›), never on the live app. Steps marked *laptop* are on a laptop browser.
You need three sign-ins: you (Owner), the test Admin, the test Crew member.
Before you start, have ready (as Owner or the test Admin):
- a **Shoot / Site Visit** for the test Crew member **today 2:00–4:00 pm**, booked by the test Admin, with a place;
- a **Meeting** for the test Crew member **in three days at 10:00 am**;
- one Crew member on **approved leave today** (a single day).

Each step: **do this → you should see this.**

## As Owner (about 5 minutes)

1. **Open Today** → after Needs you, a **Team today** card: three counts (**Free now**, **Booked today**, **On leave**), then one line per person, booked people first: "‹Crew› · Shoot / Site Visit 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · booked by ‹Admin›", then "Free all day", then "On leave". You appear only if you are booked.
2. **Tap "Show all N"** → everyone shows in place; it reads "Show fewer". **Press back** → you leave Today (opening the list added no step).
3. **Tap "+ Book someone free"** → Calendar opens on **today's Day**, with the Crew card listing who is free and "+ New task". *This is one more tap than opening New task straight from Today; we chose it to keep Today fast. Tell me if you want New task to open directly instead.* Press back → Today.
4. *(laptop)* **Open Today** → the card sits on the right, above This week. This week still shows when it has something, and disappears when empty.
5. **Tap Tasks** → between Needs you and Open tasks, **Upcoming shoots and visits**: the shoot "Wed 25 · 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · ‹place›" with the people under it, then the meeting in three days. The same tasks are **not** repeated under Open tasks.
6. **Tap People** → unchanged: no Today or Next booked columns on your People page.

## As the test Admin (about 5 minutes)

7. **Open Today** → the same Team today card. The shoot line ends "· you" (the Admin booked it).
8. **Tap Tasks** → Upcoming shoots and visits, as in step 5.
9. **Tap People** → photos, role and status as before, and two more: **Today** ("Shoot / Site Visit 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm · you" with a coloured dot, "Free all day", **"On leave · back ‹day›"**) and **Next booked** ("‹day› · Meeting 10:00 am · you", or "Nothing booked"). On the phone each card has them as two lines; nothing scrolls sideways.

## As the test Crew member (about 3 minutes)

10. **Open My Day** → the shoot (or the meeting, if it needs a note) shows its **time and place** where other tasks say "Due …".
11. **Tap Tasks** → **Upcoming shoots and visits** lists only **your own** shoots and visits; they are not repeated under Due today or Upcoming.
12. **Open People?** → the Crew have no People page (unchanged).

## The guide (about 2 minutes)

Open **Me → How MaxOff works** and read:
13. **Owner:** "See who's free today", "Read the Tasks screen" (Upcoming shoots and visits).
14. **Admin:** "See who's free today", "The Tasks screen", "People".
15. **Crew:** "Find your tasks" (your shoots and visits come first), "Read My Day" (time and place).

## Tidy up

16. Cancel the test shoot and meeting, and the test leave, if you made them for this walk.

## Last check

17. **Anything that looks different from today's app and is not on this list is a bug. Tell me.**

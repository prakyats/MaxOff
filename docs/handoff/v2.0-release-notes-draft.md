# MaxOff v2.0: fast and settled (release notes, draft)

**Not released yet.** Release candidate: `phase-v2` @ bafd6f45. The Owner tags after his phone walk.
This is the plain-words draft for `docs/RELEASE-NOTES.md`. No new feature in this release: the app is faster, and a handful of rough edges are fixed.

## What the team will notice

### Owner
- **Screens open faster.** Most of all a task, Tasks, a client, a project and Templates.
- **Today's "Needs you" stays still.** The list no longer shows an empty box and then pushes the page down. The rows keep their place from the start.
- **A client's page and a project's page load without a jump.**
- **A project on hold says "On hold" everywhere.** On the client's Overview and Projects tab the row shows an amber "On hold", never "Open". Open one of its deliverables and the sheet says at the top: "The project is on hold: ticks and Mark done wait until it is resumed." Its stages are greyed out.
- **A task on hold explains itself to you too.** The amber line now also says "Start, Done and the stage ticks wait until the task is resumed."
- **More → Appearance (phone).** The row now opens a small sheet. Tap Light, Dark or System. The sheet closes and the row shows your choice, for example "Appearance · Dark". Back then closes More.
- **Phone menus look even.** "Put on hold…", "Resume" and a few items in the project and deliverable ⋯ menus were smaller than the rest. They are the same size now.
- **Tap a chip, open the task.** A tap on a task row's chip ("2 days late", "Note it", "On hold"…) used to do nothing. Now the whole row opens the task.
- **The calendar keeps an early tap.** Tap a day while the calendar is still loading and it opens as soon as it is ready.

### Admins
- Everything in the Owner's list above, on their own clients and tasks.
- **A project the Owner put on hold is now plain to see.** Its row on the client's page reads "On hold", and its deliverables say why the ticks wait. Before, an Admin found out only when a tick was refused.
- **Today's "Client work" stays still** while it loads.
- **End day is ready sooner.**

### Crew
- **Screens open faster**, My Day most of all on a phone.
- **My Day shows "On hold · 1".** A task on hold used to disappear from My Day. Now a line under the lists says how many are on hold. Tap it to see them.
- **Tap a chip, open the task.** The whole task row opens the task, chips included.
- **The calendar keeps an early tap.** Tap a day (or the handle) while it is still loading and it answers as soon as it is ready.
- **End day is ready sooner.**
- Nothing else changes in how the Crew work. Their Appearance setting is still on Me.

### Everyone, signed out
- On the sign-in page, if the phone switches between light and dark, the colours now fade in instead of snapping. Signed in, nothing changed.

## Under the hood
1. Each screen now asks the database for everything it needs in one or two trips, and never more than six at once.
2. The first thing a screen sends already has its content, and the likely next screens are fetched ahead for every role.
3. The database's permission checks were rewritten to run faster; they give exactly the same answers (tests compare old and new).
4. Fourteen database changes, all additions. Nothing is removed or rewritten, so rolling back to v1.9 needs no extra step.
5. Flaky tests were fixed at their causes, and the speed checks now run on every change.

## Known issue
- **Settings → Thresholds:** if you press Save and type a new edit straight away, that new edit can be thrown away. Wait a moment after Save before typing again. A fix is planned in v2.1.

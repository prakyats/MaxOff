# MaxOff v3.5 "Month close": the Owner's phone walk (about 20 minutes)

Use the **installed app on your phone**, on the **preview** of this release (the Preview workflow's address for `phase-v35`), never on the live app.
You need two sign-ins: you (Owner) and the test Admin.

**Before you start, on the preview** (about 3 minutes, as Owner): open a monthly project of a client the test Admin runs, switch it to **last month** (October) and tap **"Set this month's amount"**: type 10000 and save. Make sure October has one deliverable marked done and one still open. This makes October the month you can close. Amounts are test amounts on staging; nobody but you can see them.

Each step: **do this → you should see this.** The words in quotes are the app's own.

## As Owner: the reminder and the way in (about 3 minutes)

1. **More → Reports** → under On hold, a line **"October isn't closed yet"**, with **"Close October"** under it. (If an earlier month has an amount on the preview, it names that one first, with "and 1 more month".) No amount anywhere in it.
2. **Tap the line** → the **Month close** screen for October. **Press back** → Reports.
3. **Tap Revenue, then the left arrow to October** → beside "October 2026", a small link **"Close month"**. **Go back to November** → no link (November hasn't ended).
4. **Back to October, tap "Close month"** → the Month close screen again.

## As Owner: close October (about 4 minutes)

5. **Look at "Still waiting"** → one row per kind that has something, each with a count and one line saying what it means, for example **"Carry decisions"** or **"Open work"**. No rupee amount on this screen. Under the list: "None of this stops the close: it stays open and can be settled afterwards."
6. **Tap a row (for example Carry decisions)** → it opens where that is settled. **Press back** → the close screen. (Attendance, leave, leaving early, extra work and claims open Approvals, which is not filtered to October.)
7. **At the bottom: "Close October with N things still waiting"** (N is the sum of the counts). **Tap it** → a sheet **"Close October?"** that says the report is saved as version 1 and that the things waiting can still be settled afterwards. **Press back** → only the sheet closes; nothing is closed.
8. **Tap the button again → "Close October"** → a message "October closed · v1". The screen now says **"Closed 26 Nov · v1"** (today's date), a row **"v1 · Closed 26 Nov"** with your name, and under **Changed since close: "Nothing has moved."**
9. **Press back to Revenue** → beside October's name: **"Closed 26 Nov · v1"**. Each line shows its billing status at the close, "Not billed" (or "Billed").
10. **Back to Reports** → the October line is gone (or now names the next unclosed month). November is not named until 6 Dec.
11. **Optional: on Revenue, go to a month before your first amount and tap "Close month"** → the close screen says **"Nothing to close."**, explains when a month can be closed, and has no button.

## As the test Admin (about 3 minutes)

12. **Sign in as the Admin. Open the same project, switch to October, and reopen the deliverable that was done** (with a reason) → allowed, exactly as before. Nothing on the page says the month is closed, and there is no Billing section.
13. **More → Reports** → the Admin's report, **no "isn't closed yet" line**, no Revenue row.
14. **Type `/reports/close/2026-10` into the address bar** (or open it from a link you send yourself) → the not-allowed page. Nothing says whether October is closed.

## As Owner: what moved, billing, the money sheets (about 4 minutes)

15. **Sign in as Owner. Reports → Revenue → October** → the **saved** figures are unchanged (Achieved still counts the deliverable the Admin reopened), and under the month's name a line **"Changed since close · See Month close"**.
16. **Tap it** → the close screen lists what moved: the deliverable with **"Reopened by ‹the Admin's name›"** and "Achieved ₹… → ₹0", the cycle's line with "Amount changed", and the month's totals. The header still says **v1**.
17. **Open the project's Billing section, and mark October's cycle billed** → back on **Revenue → October**, the line now reads **"Not billed at close · Billed now"**.
18. **On the project, October, open a money sheet** (the cycle's amount, or "Set price" of an added deliverable) → the sheet says **"October is closed. Its report changes only when you correct it."** **Press back** → only the sheet closes. (Saving would still work; the saved report would not move.)

## As Owner: correct October (about 3 minutes)

19. **Close screen → "Correct October"** → a sheet: **"What moved since the close"** with the same lines, and a box **"Why you're correcting it"** (10 to 500 characters; only you see it). **Press back** → only the sheet closes.
20. **Open it again, type "short", tap "Save version 2"** → the box says "Write a note of 10 to 500 characters." and nothing is saved.
21. **Type a real reason ("Reel reopened after the close.") → "Save version 2"** → "October corrected · v2". The screen says **"Closed 26 Nov · v2"**, a row **"v2 · Corrected 26 Nov"** with **"Corrected: ‹your note›"**, and v1 is still listed under it. Changed since close: "Nothing has moved."
22. **Tap "Correct October" again, write a note, tap "Save version 3"** → **"Nothing changed."** in the sheet, and nothing is saved. Tap Cancel.
23. **Press back** → Revenue (October, "Closed 26 Nov · v2", the "Changed since close" line gone). **Back again** → Reports. **Back again** → Today.

## Last, as Owner: the guide (about 1 minute)

24. **More → Help → "How MaxOff works" → Revenue** → **"Close a month"** and **"Correct a closed month"** match what you just did, with the close screen's picture.

## Choices made while building: tell the advisor if any should be different

From the close screen (U4):
- The waiting rows open: Approvals (attendance, leave, leaving early, extra work, expense claims; **not filtered to the month**), the deliverables list (open work), the carry screen (carry decisions), and the month's Revenue page (no amount set, ready to complete). A kind at 0 is not shown; with nothing at all the screen says "Nothing is waiting."
- The headline shows the **first close's day with the latest version**: "Closed 26 Nov · v2".
- The Correct sheet's button reads **"Save version N"**. When no revenue line moved it says expense totals and the month summary are compared too.
- "Who changed it" ("Reopened by Ravi") is shown **only for deliverables**. A changed amount, override, billing or advance says "Amount changed" without a name (they are your own edits).
- A correction compares the **whole** month (revenue, expense totals, month summary) and counts a changed **note** (override, billing, advance) as a change; a rename alone is "Nothing changed."

From a closed month on Revenue (U5):
- "Needs a look" on a closed month is **live** (today's list of what to fix), while the figures, cards and line statuses are the saved ones.
- Billing reads "Billed" / "Not billed", and **"Not billed at close · Billed now"** only when they differ. An open month shows no billing status.
- "Changed since close · See Month close" on Revenue is a quick check of the page's own figures: it never shows by mistake, but a change to a deliverable that moves no figure is listed only on the close screen.
- The closed line is on the amount, price and advance sheets, not on the category sheet or a retainer's amount (which applies from the current cycle on); for advances it follows the date you pick. There is no override sheet yet, so no line there.
- If a closed month's saved report cannot load, Revenue shows its error ("Try again") rather than today's figures, which could be taken for the saved ones.

From the reminder (U6):
- **The 6th is per month:** on 3 Dec an unclosed October is named (its 6th was 6 Nov), November only from 6 Dec.
- A month after your first priced month with no amount of its own still counts (it can be closed).
- The second line reads **"Close September"** (it names the month when there are several); the line sits under On hold.
- The year is named only when it is not this year's ("December 2026 isn't closed yet" in January).
- If the reminder cannot load, Reports shows no line rather than an error (Revenue's month header still links to the close screen).

## Three questions for you to confirm (the advisor's "owner to confirm")

1. **The weekly digest.** Decision 19 says "one line in the 08:00 digest", but since 6.5 your digest is **weekly**. The line is the last one under "Now" in the weekly email, sent on or after the 6th, and the Reports line is the daily reminder. **A week with nothing else to tell you sends no email for this line alone.** Is that right, or should the line alone send the weekly email?
2. **Is ₹0 ("Not billable") "priced"?** A month counts from the first month with any amount set, and ₹0 counts as set. So a ₹0 retainer cycle makes its month closeable and remindable. Is that right?
3. **What is on the waiting list.** Attendance, leave, **leaving early** (requests never answered; added since the kickoff), extra work, expense claims, **open work** (the month's deliverables neither done nor closed; in place of "items not approved", which no longer exists since Mark done approves), carry decisions, no amount set and ready to complete. **Staff tasks still open are left out** (decision 16 does not name them). An ended cycle's open deliverable is counted once, under carry decisions. Is that the list you want?

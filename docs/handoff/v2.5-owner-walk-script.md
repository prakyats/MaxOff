# MaxOff v2.5 "Revenue every day": the Owner's phone walk (about 15 minutes)

Use the **installed app on your phone**, on the **preview** of this release (`https://phase-v25-revenue-daily-maxoff-staging.pixoraclips.workers.dev`), never on the live app.
You need two sign-ins: you (Owner) and the test Admin.
Before you start, have ready on the preview: one client run by the test Admin with **a monthly project with an amount** (a few deliverables this month), **a monthly project with no amount set**, and **a one-time project with an amount**.

Amounts here are test amounts on staging. Nobody but you can see them.

Each step: **do this → you should see this.**

## Two answers of yours this build follows (tell us if you want either changed)

- **"Mark all" skips the lines with nothing to bill.** A line that reads "No amount set" or ₹0 "Not billable" is left out of "Mark all N billed"; a line on hold is included. A line you billed on another screen meanwhile keeps its own date and note.
- **Today's figures load on tap.** The Today line shows no amount until you tap it; the figures are fetched at that moment and are never part of Today itself (so a screenshot, the app's cache or a preloaded Today holds none).

## As Owner: Today (about 3 minutes)

1. **Open Today and scroll to the end of the main list** → the last line there reads "‹Month› · ₹ ••• achieved of ₹ ••• · Show", with "Open Revenue" beside it. On the phone "This week" comes after it.
2. **Tap the line** → a short grey bar in the same box, then "‹Month› · ₹… achieved of ₹… · Hide", with "n not priced" and "n with no amount" when there are any. Nothing on the page moves.
3. **Tap it again** → the amounts are hidden. **Tap once more** → they show straight away.
4. **Press back** → you leave Today as usual (the tap is not a step to go back through). **Open Today again** → the amounts are hidden again.
5. **Tap "Open Revenue"** → the Revenue page for this month. **Press back** → Today.

## As Owner: a project's Billing (about 6 minutes)

6. **Open the monthly project with an amount, scroll to Billing** → "Billing · Not billed", "Achieved", "Planned deliverables · n" and "Billing history".
7. **Tap "Mark billed", keep today, note "Invoice 1", save** → "Billed on ‹today› · Invoice 1".
8. **Tap "Adjust", type a figure and a note, save** → "Owner adjusted · ‹note›" with "System calculated ₹…" beside it. **"Back to calculated"** with a note → the calculated figure again.
9. **Open "Planned deliverables", give one its own value** → the others share the rest of the month's amount.
10. **Open "Billing history"** → each change you just made, newest first, with your name and the time.
11. **As the test Admin (another phone or browser), add a deliverable to this month** → you (Owner) get "‹project›: a deliverable isn't priced", with no amount. Tap it → this month's Billing.

## As Owner: the Revenue page (about 4 minutes)

12. **Reports → Revenue** → while it loads, a grey outline with the figures and a "Mark all" row; when it arrives nothing moves.
13. **Look at the lines** → each says "Billed ‹day›" or "Not billed"; an adjusted one says "Adjusted"; a held project shows "On hold ₹…" inside Remaining.
14. **Look at "Mark all N billed"** → N counts the lines not billed yet **with an amount**: the project with no amount set is not counted.
15. **Tap it** → the sheet says lines with no amount set or at ₹0 are left out. **Press back** → only the sheet closes. **Tap it again and confirm** → "N marked billed"; the project you billed in step 7 keeps "Billed on ‹that day›" and its note.

## As the test Admin (about 2 minutes)

16. **Open Today** → no revenue line, no ₹ anywhere.
17. **Open the monthly project** → no Billing section, no ₹, no "Billed" or "Billing history".
18. **Reports** → no Revenue row.

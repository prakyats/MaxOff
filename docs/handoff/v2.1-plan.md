## 5. Everything that changes at the end of v2.1
Items marked **(if Qn)** follow the decision sheet's recommended answer. They change if the owner picks another option.

### Screens: Admins
- **Calendar → Month:**
  - looks the same;
  - a day's pop-up lists **Busy** ("Asha · Shoot · 2–4 pm · booked by Nikhil"), **On leave** and **Free**, instead of "Everyone is available";
  - "Everyone is available" shows only when that's true.
- **Calendar → Day:** the hour grid is replaced. The day's events and due tasks go on the left; a **Crew panel** (Busy, On leave, Free, "+ New task on ‹day›") on the right.
- **Calendar → Week:** the seven-column hour grid is replaced by **one row per day**: Events · due, Crew busy, On leave, Free. Clicking a day opens it.
- **Today:**
  - a **Team today** card replaces the This week box: free now, booked today, on leave, one line per person, and "+ Book someone free";
  - Needs you, Client work and My tasks are unchanged.
- **Tasks:**
  - a new **Upcoming shoots and visits** section shows each event's date and time, place and people (not "Due 6:00 pm");
  - Needs you and Open tasks are unchanged.
- **New task:**
  - **Type** is the first field, in Settings order;
  - a shoot, site visit, meeting or posting shows **When / From / To / Where / Client** up front, and Done by defaults to the end of the event;
  - any other type shows one deadline;
  - **Who's on it** lists people under **Free then / Busy then**, with a "Clashes" label naming who booked them.
- **People:** photos, role and status stay; **Today** ("Shoot 2–4 pm · you", "Free all day", "On leave · back Tue 17") and **Next booked** are added.
- **Phone:** the same changes in the phone layout. The Day view's "Crew busy" sits after the Admin's own tasks; New task's Who's on it is a sheet.

### Screens: Owner
- **Calendar:** the same Month day pop-up, Day and Week as the Admins'. You still see everything in full.
- **Today:** gets the **Team today** card too.
- **Tasks:** Upcoming shoots and visits, as for Admins.
- **New task:** the type-first form and the Free/Busy picker.
- **People:** unchanged for you.

### Screens: Crew
- **Tasks / My Day:** their own shoots and visits show with the time and place.
- **Every role:** screens draw their outline at once on a tap, after the app reopens too: the bottom tabs, a client, a project, a person.
- **Nothing else changes for the Crew.** They still see nobody else's bookings.

### Screens: everyone, small fixes
- **Appearance:** the phone's More → Appearance and the laptop's menu read the same (labels, order, the tick).
- **Thresholds:** an edit typed straight after Save is no longer lost.
- **Stay until:** you can set it ahead of time from a person's page.
- **Calendar:** faster to finish loading; a tap while it loads is answered sooner.
- **Accessibility:** contrast and labels fixed where the axe checks flag them, such as the dim other-month days.
- **Bad network (if Q1–Q3):**
  - a create that loses its signal can be tapped again **without making a duplicate**;
  - Start day / End day with no network say "No network, tap again";
  - a late start caused by no network is pre-filled with "No network, first tried 9:58" for the Owner to judge.
- **Two people editing the same thing (if Q4):** the second save is stopped only if both changed the same detail. They see who changed it and choose "Use theirs" or "Save mine".

### Permissions and data
- **What Admins can see, newly:**
  - the **task type** and **who booked it** on other people's bookings;
  - the **Owner's** bookings shown the same way;
  - **date-only events** count as busy all day.
- **Still never shown:** another person's task title, client or location.
- **Crew:** no new visibility. **The Owner:** unchanged (already sees everything).
- **How it's built:**
  - one expand-only migration widens `member_availability()` (or adds a sibling function) under the existing `availability.view` key;
  - no new permission key;
  - pgTAP for Owner, Admin and Crew, allowed and denied.
- **Rule change, WORKFLOWS §4 overlap warning:** a date-only event now clashes with a timed event that day.
- **Data change:** availability counts the viewer's own bookings (today it skips them, which is the "Everyone is available" bug).
- **(If Q3) repeat-safe creates:** a new internal ledger table (`request_keys`, no API access) and "_once" versions of the create functions. Nothing is removed.
- **(If Q4) edit clashes:** a version check on tasks, projects, deliverables and clients.
- **Security hardening:**
  - the v2.0 read-folds get explicit own-row filters;
  - the diagnostics pages get a rate limit or token.
- **No money, Realtime or export change.** Revenue stays Owner-only.

### Behind the scenes (nobody sees these)
- **Real-user speed:** sent from the team's phones to Sentry, per screen and role. Nothing personal is sent.
- **New test suites:** iPhone (WebKit), Slow 4G and accessibility (axe) run on every change.
- **The "first screen in six reads" rule** extended to all routes.
- **The 10:00 rush test (if Q5–Q7):** on staging only, never the live app.
- **Uploaded-files backup (if Q8–Q9):** a nightly locked copy of logos, photos and receipt pictures in a second storage box, kept always.
- **The restore drill (if Q10–Q11):** on your laptop with last night's backup, then every three months with a reminder.

### What does not change
Roles, money rules, attendance and leave decisions (still yours alone), client work, approvals,
notifications, the Month calendar's layout, the Owner's People page, and Crew navigation.

MaxOff: how the app works

A plain-language guide to the finished app, as decided up to 1 October 2026: who does what, where every screen is, and exactly what happens after each tap. For Pixora Clips. This edition is for version 1.2: crew tasks and notifications join stage 1 at app.maxoff.in.

PrishitOwner. Decides attendance, leave, money and every final approval.
PrakyatAdmin. Runs his assigned clients and checks the work he gives out. Also an employee.
XyzEmployee (Crew). Does the tasks given to them, marks the working day, takes leave.

How to read this guide. Screens are written as tap paths, for example More → Clients → New client. Each section is tagged Ready now (live in the app at https://app.maxoff.in) or Phase N (decided, arriving with that phase). Small details of a screen may change while it is built; the rules in this guide are the decisions.

Live with version 1.2 at https://app.maxoff.in: attendance, leave, comp leave, expenses, the month summary, clients and people (stage 1), and now crew tasks with freelancers and notifications: on the phone, in Alerts and on the bell, with the few emails and quiet hours. Screens from later phases are already in the app and say "coming soon" until they arrive. Next, in this order (crew first; each stage goes live as soon as it is ready): the rest of 5, reminders · 6 My Day, Today, calendar, end-of-day report · 8 handing in work · 7 client projects · 9 money and reports · 10 polish and full launch. A tag like Phase 6 means "live once phase 6 is released".

1The app at a glance

MaxOff is Pixora Clips' internal operations system. Clients never log in. It runs on phones and computers, installs like an app, and works in Indian time (IST) everywhere.

Who sees what

Prishit (Owner)Today · Approvals · Tasks · Calendar · More
More holds Reports, Clients, People, Settings and Me.		Nothing is hidden from Prishit. He is exempt from marking attendance.
Prakyat (Admin)Today · Approvals · Tasks · Calendar · More

Tasks and Alerts are live for everyone, and Prakyat's Approvals holds the tasks he checks. Calendar and Prakyat's Reports are in place and say, in plain words, what they will be for and that they are coming soon. On a phone, everyone has a bell at the top of each screen; it opens Alerts and shows how many are unread. Prishit is never given a task himself.

The colour rule. The one solid red button on a screen is the one that saves or commits something (Save, Submit, Request leave, the confirming button). A red outline means "careful, this removes or cancels something", and it always asks you to confirm with a button that says exactly what it does ("Deactivate Xyz"), never just "OK". Grey buttons go back or open something.

2Getting in and staying in

Installing the app Ready now

  1. Open https://app.maxoff.in on the phone: in Chrome on Android, in Safari on iPhone. Sign in.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Install app (Android) or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone). On iPhone this step is required for notifications.
  3. MaxOff now opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app. On iPhone, sign in once more inside the app the first time (the installed app keeps its own sign-in).
  4. Turn on notifications: a new joiner's welcome screen walks through it (below); anyone can tap the slim band above the bottom bar, "Notifications are off · Turn on", then Turn on and Allow (section 14).

Joining the team Ready now

  1. Prishit invites a person: More → People → Add person → Employee, with email, name, role (Admin or Crew) and job title → Send invite. The invite goes to the person by email (from notifications@mail.maxoff.in), and the same link appears with Copy: Prishit sends it on WhatsApp together with the one-page "Your first day with MaxOff". A Freelancer is added from the same button, with no invite (section 13).
  2. Xyz opens the link on the phone, taps Continue to MaxOff (only that tap uses the link up, so a chat preview cannot), sets a password and lands on a welcome screen on Me, with a short card "Get notifications": on an iPhone in Safari first Install MaxOff (four numbered steps with pictures: Share → Add to Home Screen → Add, then open it from the icon and sign in again: the card carries on there), then Turn on notifications, then Send a test and "Did it arrive? Yes / No". Later skips it for good; the band keeps reminding until notifications work. Until the card is finished or skipped, every sign-in, on any phone or computer, opens on this welcome screen (a link to a particular screen still opens that screen); a test that reaches a device finishes it, whether sent from the card, the band or Me → Help. People who joined before this card existed never see it. The link works once, within 24 hours, whichever is opened first, the email or the copied link; if it has expired or was used, Prishit taps People → Xyz → ⋯ → Copy invite link for a fresh one.
  3. Attendance starts the next day: the joining day is never counted.

Staying signed in Ready now

  • Once signed in, people stay signed in, like WhatsApp or Gmail. The app never signs anyone out on its own, so tasks and notifications reach them in the evening and on days off too.
  • Opening the app is not attendance. Attendance is the separate Start day and End day (section 3).
  • Me → Sign out of this device is only for a lost or shared phone. It warns that notifications stop on that device.
  • When Prishit deactivates someone (People → person → ⋯ → Deactivate), they lose access on every device at once. Their history is kept.
  • Forgot password: Sign-in screen → Forgot your password? emails a link (from noreply@mail.maxoff.in, "Set your MaxOff password"): Continue to MaxOff, then a new password.

The Me page Ready now

Shows your name, phone, sign-in email, role and job title as plain text. To change them: Me → ✎ Edit → change a field → Save (red) → a confirmation names the change ("Your name will change from X to Y") → confirm → "Saved". Leaving with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?". Me also holds Extra work & expenses (and, for Prakyat, Attendance & leave; Xyz has it as the Leave tab), your photo, Appearance (light, dark or follow the phone), Notifications for this device, Your devices (section 14), and at the bottom Help & troubleshooting: Send a test notification (section 14), Reload app and the app's version and, for a coordinator, Your freelancers (section 13).

3Xyz's working day Ready now

Working hours are flexible. MaxOff records when the day really starts and ends, and never guesses.

Morning: Start day

On a working day Xyz opens the app anywhere, any time. Until the day is started, this appears inside the app:

┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ │ Started working? │ │ Start your day to record it. │ │ │ │ [ Start day ] (red) │ │ On leave today? Choose leave │ │ Just looking │ └──────────────────────────────────────┘
  • Start day → the start time is recorded at that tap. The day shows "Present · waiting for approval" until Prishit approves it.
  • On leave today? → choose Leave or Half day, add an optional reason, submit. It goes to Prishit.
  • Just looking → the prompt closes (for example Xyz only wanted to check tasks). It returns when the app is opened or returned to, at most every 30 minutes, until the day is started or leave chosen. My Day also shows a strip: "Not started · Start day".

During the day: My Day Phase 6

● Started 9:12 am · waiting for approval [End day] Needs your "Task Noted" 2 Changes requested 1 Overdue 1 Today 3 Upcoming · Events [ + Suggest a task ] End day

Everything is one tap away: tapping a line opens those tasks; tapping the status line opens Attendance & leave. For now My Day is the status line with Start day and End day, and below it "More of your day is coming soon"; the tasks themselves are on the Tasks tab (section 9).

Evening: End day

  1. Tap My Day → End day.
  2. Optional: Worked late? Add an overtime note (roughly how long, and what was worked on).
  3. "Any expenses to claim today?" → No (the default), or Yes: the button becomes End day, add expenses and the claim form opens right after (section 17).
  4. Confirm. The end time is recorded. End day is final for that day: it cannot be reopened. The app stays fully usable afterwards (checking tomorrow's tasks at 9 PM is fine and is not work).

Days off (Sundays and holidays)

No prompt and no absence check. If Xyz actually worked, they tap My Day → I worked today and describe the work. Prishit then decides (section 5).

No prompt. The strip says "On leave today" with I'm working today, which asks to confirm. If Xyz confirms and Prishit approves, the day counts as "1 day worked" and the leave itself stays as it was. On an approved half day the strip says "Half day today" and Start day and End day stay available: the half day stands.

Prakyat (Admin) does exactly the same as Xyz; his Start day and End day live on his Today screen. Prishit never marks attendance.

4Leave Ready now

Asking for leave (Xyz or Prakyat)

  1. Leave (Xyz's tab; for Prakyat Me → Attendance & leave, or the status line on Today) → Request leave.
  2. Choose Leave (one day or a range), Half day (one date) or Comp leave (only shown when a comp credit is available, section 5). Dates can be today or later, up to a year long. The reason is optional.
  3. Tap Request leave (red). It appears as "Waiting" and in Prishit's Approvals.
  • Withdraw a request while it's still waiting: tap it → Withdraw → confirm.
  • Change or cancel approved leave: tap it → Change / Ask to cancel. This sends a new request to Prishit; the original stays approved until he decides. Possible only until the leave has ended; after that, only Prishit can correct it.
  • Overlapping requests are refused ("request a change instead").
  • The Attendance tab shows each day of the month with its full story in plain words ("You chose Present", "Prishit changed it to Absent: …" with his reason).
Only Prishit decides leave. Prakyat has no say in anyone's attendance or leave, including his own.

5Extra work and comp leave Ready now

Recording extra work (Xyz)

  • Overtime note: Me → Extra work & expenses → Extra work → Add note (or at End day). Pick the day (up to 7 days back), roughly how long (optional) and what was worked on (required).
  • Worked on a day off: My Day → I worked today on a Sunday or holiday (or later, up to 7 days back), with a note about the work.

Prishit reviews it

Approvals → Extra work → Review → Decide…. For each note: Grant 1 day, Grant ½ day or No comp leave. For day-off work he also decides whether to mark the day as worked. Nothing is automatic: if the extra work came from Xyz's own mistake, for example, Prishit simply grants nothing. Xyz sees the outcome on the note: "1 comp leave granted · use by 31 Oct" or "Reviewed by the Owner".

Prishit can also grant comp leave at any time, for any reason, without a note: People → Xyz → Leave → Grant comp leave (½ or 1 day, optional note Xyz sees). A double tap grants it once. He can take back an unused grant with Revoke, with a reason Xyz reads.

Using comp leave (Xyz)

  1. Xyz sees the balance on Me → Extra work & expenses → Extra work: "1½ days of comp leave · use by 31 Oct", and under his leave requests on Leave: "Comp leave: 1½ days · use by 31 Oct".
  2. Request leave → Comp leave (1 day) or Comp leave, half day (½) → pick the date from the list. The list holds only working days (no Sundays, no holidays) up to the end of the month the credit was granted in.
  3. It goes to Prishit like any leave. He can refuse it (for example, there's a shoot that day). While it waits, the credit is held; if refused, withdrawn or cancelled, the credit comes back (unless its month has ended).
Credits expire at the end of the calendar month they were granted in, even if that's two days later. Expired credits stay visible in the history. If Prishit later adds a holiday on a date that already has comp leave (waiting or approved), that comp leave is cancelled and the credit goes back to Xyz, with a note saying which holiday.

6Prishit's attendance tools

Today Ready now (full screen in phase 6)

Today's attendance › 11 Present · 2 On leave · 1 Waiting · 1 Not started Xyz ● Waiting for decision › Abc ● Not started yet › … ● Present ›

The card opens Approvals. Under it is the board: everyone expected today, each once, in the order Prishit acts on them (waiting for his decision, not started, present, on leave, absent). Tapping a person opens their page. Below, "More of your day is coming soon": the short "Needs you" list, tasks and client work join in phase 6.

Approving attendance and leave Ready now

Approvals is one screen, grouped: Attendance · Leave · Extra work · Expenses · Crew tasks (Client items join in phase 7). The badge on the tab shows how many things wait. Crew tasks are covered in section 9; Prakyat's Approvals holds only his Tasks to check. Extra work and Expenses are review-only: every one is opened and decided on its own.

  • Approve one row → it fades, with a 6-second Undo. Nothing is saved until the Undo time passes (or the app is left).
  • Approve all N on a group → a confirmation with the count → "Approve N" (red). Only the rows on screen are approved.
  • Review → a sheet with the details → Correct the day (choose the right status and write a reason) or Reject leave (with a reason). The form says "Xyz will see this reason".
  • Correcting a leave day to Present counts it as "1 day worked"; the approved leave stays, and a note says so.
  • Approving leave that covers days Prishit has already decided leaves those days as he decided, and says so ("These days keep your earlier decision").

A person's page Ready now

Today → tap a person or More → People → tap a person. Tabs: Profile (details, Edit, and ⋯ for Deactivate, Reactivate, Copy invite link), Leave (their requests, with Edit and Cancel on approved leave), Attendance (month by month, with Correct on every day). The ‹ at the top goes back.

The month summary Ready now

People → person → Month, and the team report at More → Reports → Month, live at any time (section 16).

7Clients Ready now

Adding a client (Prishit)

  1. More → Clients → New client: name (required, unique), and anything else known: business name, address, GSTIN, phone, email, website, requirements.
  2. The client opens in Draft. Add contacts (one is marked primary), the Brand kit (logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice, notes), the Google Drive folder link and any custom fields.
  3. ⋯ → Assign Admin → Prakyat. Prakyat is notified: "You now run Sharma Weddings".
  4. ⋯ → Activate. The client is live.

The client page

Tabs: Overview (details, contacts, custom fields) · Brand · Activity (everything that changed); Projects joins in phase 7. The crew tasks labelled with a client are found under Tasks → All tasks, filtered by client. Prishit also sees his private notes and, later, money panels.

  • Pause: everything stays visible and editable, but monthly work stops creating new rounds. Close (Inactive): stays searchable, no new work until Prishit reactivates it. Only Prishit changes the state.
  • Prakyat sees and edits only the clients assigned to him. Xyz never sees a client's page, only the client's name and brand basics on a task.
  • The client list: cards with logo, name, state and Admin; search and filters; opens on Active clients.
  • Brand colours are swatches: each colour shows as a chip with its name and hex code, and a tap copies the code ("Copied #E11D48"). Fonts are a simple list, each with an optional note on where it's used. In Brand → Edit each colour is a row with a colour picker, a name and the hex code; rows can be added, moved up or down and removed, and Save names every change.
  • Closing a client asks for an optional reason. Only Prishit ever sees it, on the client's Activity.
  • No client is ever left without an Admin. If Prishit makes Prakyat Crew, or deactivates him, the same confirmation first asks "Move Prakyat's 3 clients to: [Admin]" (one Admin for all, or Choose per client), then makes the change.

8Client projects and the monthly round Phase 7

Every client's work is organised as projects, and each project is made of pieces of work (items), for example "Reel 1 – Ambience".

Sharma Weddings ├── Monthly Production every month · Retainer │ └── October: Reel 1 – Ambience · Reel 2 – Menu · … · Post 1 · Post 2 ├── Drone Documentation every week · Retainer │ └── Week 1 · Week 2 · … └── Brand Film one-time · Additional Work └── Make the video

Creating a project (Prakyat or Prishit)

  1. Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → New project (or start from a saved template).
  2. Name it, choose how it repeats (monthly, weekly or one-time), optionally pick the steps every piece goes through (for example Script → Shoot → Edit → Posted), and list the pieces.
  3. Prishit only sets the money: an amount per month (split across the pieces, or a value per piece) or a fixed amount for a one-time project, and the category (Retainer, Project or Additional Work). Prakyat never sees any of it.

The monthly round

  • On the 1st (or every Monday for weekly work), MaxOff creates the new round for every active client, copying the list of pieces. Prakyat renames them for the month's themes.
  • The project page shows the round (switch between months), each piece with its steps, and progress like "9/12 done · 8/12 approved".
  • Prakyat ticks steps as the work happens and marks a piece done when it's delivered. Several pieces can be ticked at once.
Planned: a place on Prakyat's and Prishit's Today that shows every client's pieces due this week and overdue, so daily work isn't four screens deep. Settled at the start of phase 7.

9Crew tasks, from creation to final approval Ready now

Tasks are how work is given to people. They are separate from client projects: Xyz sees tasks, never projects. Everyone has a Tasks tab, and its badge counts the tasks you haven't noted yet and the ones sent back to you for changes.

Creating a task (Prakyat or Prishit)

  1. Tasks → New task. Start from fills it from a saved template (type, priority, stages and field defaults); the people, the deadline and the client are always picked by hand.
  2. Fill in: Title, Type (Normal, Shoot / Site Visit, Meeting, Posting, Review / Approval, Other, Custom; Prishit edits the list), Priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), Assign to (add the people, then pick the primary owner: the one who marks it done), Deadline (date and time), an optional Client label (Sharma Weddings), a description (links to a brief go here) and optional stages to tick off.
  3. MaxOff warns under a person's name, and never blocks: "4 tasks already due on 3 Oct", "Busy 10:00 AM–12:00 PM on 3 Oct", "On leave on 3 Oct". Keeping the person anyway is recorded.
  4. Create task (red). It appears in Xyz's Tasks at once, and Xyz is notified (section 14).

Shoots, site visits, meetings and postings also ask for an event date, optional start and end times, a place and a purpose. They will show on the Calendar when it arrives (phase 6).

Xyz receives it and does the work

  1. The task is at the top of Tasks, under Not noted. Xyz opens it and taps Task Noted (red), which records that it was seen, and when. Every person on the task does this.
  2. The button becomes Start work: tap it when the work begins, and the task reads "In progress". It is optional: ⋯ → Mark done works without it.
  3. While working, Xyz ticks the stages and writes in Chat.
  4. When it's finished, only the primary owner taps Mark done. A sheet opens with an optional note: paste the Google Drive link to the work there (a file or a folder shared with whoever checks it). There is no file upload yet (section 10), so the link in the note is the hand-in. If the deadline has passed, "Why is it late?" must be answered. Then Mark done (red).
  5. The task goes for review and is locked for the people on it ("Locked while it's with the reviewers. Chat stays open.") until it is approved or sent back.
  • If nobody notes it: the task shows under Needs you on Prakyat's Tasks after 4 hours and on Prishit's after 8 hours ("Xyz hasn't noted it · 9 h"). Prishit sets both in Settings → Thresholds. Automatic reminders to Xyz arrive with the next part of phase 5.
  • A task is overdue the moment its deadline passes without final approval: it moves to Overdue in My tasks and to Needs you for Prakyat and Prishit.

10Handing in work (files and links) Phase 8

Until phase 8 nothing is uploaded: the hand-in is the note on Mark done, with a Google Drive link pasted into it (section 9). This is what arrives then:

  1. On the task: Hand in work.
  2. Photos and documents (up to 25 MB) and short videos (up to 100 MB) upload straight from the phone or computer. Photos are kept exactly as taken, full quality, including iPhone formats; MaxOff makes a small preview so they open anywhere.
  3. Large videos: paste a Google Drive link to the file. If a file is too big to upload, MaxOff says so and asks for a link.
  4. Each hand-in is a version (v1, v2 …). Prakyat and Prishit preview it, download the original, comment, and approve or ask for changes on that exact version.
For the first version, a pasted Drive link points to Xyz's own file: if they delete or unshare it, it's gone. The automatic company copy in Pixora's Google Drive comes after the launch (section 21).

11Turning finished work into earned money Phases 7 and 9

A task being approved does not tick the client's piece of work by itself. The two are confirmed separately, on purpose:

  1. Xyz's task "Edit Reel 1" is approved (section 9).
  2. Prakyat opens Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → Monthly Production, ticks the steps (Edit ✓, Posted ✓) and marks Reel 1 done.
  3. Prishit sees it in Approvals → Client items and taps Approve (one or several), or sends it back to Prakyat with a reason.
  4. Only after Prishit's approval does Reel 1's value count as earned. Until then it is only "planned".

Prishit's money view (Reports, and panels on each client) shows for each client, category and month: Potential (planned), Achieved (approved) and Remaining. He can adjust a figure with a note; the original calculation is always kept beside it.

12Additional work Phase 7

The client asks for something outside the monthly package, for example one extra reel:

  1. Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → New project: "Extra Reel – Engagement", one-time.
  2. Prishit sets its category to Additional Work and gives it a price.
  3. From here it follows the same path: a task for Xyz, hand-in, Prakyat's check, Prishit's approval, Prakyat marks the piece done, Prishit approves it, and it counts as earned.

Keeping it as its own project means the monthly package's numbers stay clean, and Additional Work shows separately in the reports.

13Freelancers Ready now

  • Freelancers don't get a login. Prishit adds one with People → Add person → Freelancer: name, job title, optional phone, and a coordinator (an employee, Admin or Crew, for example Xyz; never Prishit) → Add freelancer. One person can coordinate several freelancers.
  • Tasks can be given to the freelancer (say, Asha) like anyone else; in every list she reads "Asha (freelancer)". Xyz, as coordinator, does everything for her from Xyz's own account: the task is in Xyz's My tasks marked "For Asha", and the buttons read Noted for Asha, Start work for Asha, Mark done for Asha. In Chat, Xyz chooses "Writing as: Yourself" or "For Asha". The record always names both: "Noted by Xyz for Asha".
  • Notifications meant for the freelancer go to Xyz, marked "· for Asha" (section 14). Xyz's own freelancers are listed on Me → Your freelancers.
  • If Prakyat checks a task and also coordinates a freelancer on it, his check is skipped, as if he were on the task himself (section 9).
  • On the freelancer's page Prishit can Change coordinator (the history is kept) or Invite as employee: the same record gets a login, and attendance starts the day after joining. Deactivating a coordinator first asks who takes over their freelancers.
  • No attendance, leave or expense claims for freelancers, and they are left out of the month summary. Tasks → All tasks can be filtered to employees or freelancers; separate figures in the reports come in phases 6 and 9. Payments to freelancers are not in the first version.

14Notifications

MaxOff tells the people concerned the moment something happens, as a notification on the phone (or the computer), also when the app is closed. Notifications are mandatory: nothing in the app turns them off.

Turning them on Ready now

  1. Install the app first (section 2). On iPhone this is required: an iPhone notifies only the installed app, so in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open MaxOff from its icon. Until then the band reads "Install MaxOff to get notifications · How", and tapping it shows these steps.
  2. Until a notification has really reached one of your devices, a slim band sits just above the bottom bar on every screen: "Notifications are off · Turn on". Tap it.
  3. A sheet opens, "Turn on notifications", with a line on what they are for. Tap Turn on (red); the phone then asks for permission: tap Allow. "Notifications are on for this device" appears, and the band now reads "Check notifications reach you · Send a test".
  4. Tap it → Send a test (red). "MaxOff notifications are on" arrives within seconds, quiet hours or not, and the band is gone on all your devices.
  5. Check it any time: Me → Help & troubleshooting → Send a test notification → Send test. MaxOff then asks "Did it arrive?": No opens what to check on that phone or browser (iPhone: the installed app and Settings → Notifications → MaxOff; Android: the site's notifications, battery and Do Not Disturb; a computer: the site permission and the system's notification settings; Brave: its Google push setting). Nothing is saved either way.
  • The band has no close button: it stays, for everyone including Prishit, until a notification (a test or a real one) has reached one of your devices, and comes back whenever you can't be reached, saying why: "Notifications are off" (never turned on), "Notifications are blocked" (permission taken back on a device), "Install MaxOff to get notifications" (an iPhone without the app) or "Notifications aren't reaching you" (repeated delivery failures: tap it and send a test).
  • Tapped "Don't allow"? Tap the band: the sheet reads "Notifications are blocked on this device": allow notifications for MaxOff in the browser's site settings (or the app's info screen), then come back.
  • A second device, for example the laptop: Me → Notifications → Turn on here.
  • Me → Sign out of this device stops notifications on that device only. Closing the app does not.

Your devices Ready now

  • Me → Your devices lists every phone and computer that gets your notifications, in plain words ("Chrome on Android", "Safari on iPhone", "Edge on Windows"), with "installed app" or "browser" and when it last got one ("Last notification 2 h ago", "No notification yet"). One that stopped says why: "Notifications were turned off on this device" or "Stopped after repeated failures".
  • The phone in your hand is marked This device; to stop it, use Sign out of this device above.
  • Any other device has Remove (red outline): a confirmation asks, with a red button naming it, Remove Chrome on Android. Notifications stop there and it leaves the list; that device is not signed out. It stays off, even when it is opened again. To get notifications on it again, open MaxOff on that device and tap Turn on there (the band, or Me → Notifications).
  • Nobody else sees this list or can remove your devices, Prishit included: in Settings → Notifications he sees only whether you can be reached, the kind of device and when a notification last worked.

What arrives Ready now

  • The full text shows on the lock screen, for example "New task: Edit Reel 1 – Ambience", "Assigned by Prakyat · due 3 Oct, 18:00". Amounts never appear in a notification, not even Prishit's.
  • Tapping it opens the right screen (the task, your leave, Approvals), with its list underneath, so back goes to the list and not out of the app.
  • Nobody is notified about their own action, and nobody outside the matter is told.
  • A freelancer's notifications go to their coordinator, marked "· for Asha".

The bell and Alerts Ready now

Quiet hours: 22:00 to 07:00 Ready now

  • Between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM no phone notification is sent, whatever it is about. There is no "urgent" exception; only the test from Me goes through.

  • The hours are the same for everyone for now; a setting for Prishit comes later.

What also comes by email Ready now

  • Never: comments, task edits and everything else. Those are notifications only.

  • The invite (from the same address) and password emails are separate and always sent.

16End of the month

Unfinished client work Phase 7

MaxOff highlights every piece not finished in the month's round. For each one Prishit chooses:

  • Carry forward: it moves into next month but keeps its original month and value, so next month's package isn't inflated.
  • Close (with a reason): kept, never deleted; its value stays visible as "not achieved".
  • Leave pending.

Billing and closing the month Phase 9

  • Prishit marks each client's month Billed (optional date and note). MaxOff doesn't send invoices.
  • Reports → Close month saves a permanent record of the month. Later mistakes are fixed as dated corrections, never by overwriting.

17Expense claims Ready now

Claiming (Xyz or Prakyat)

  1. At End day: "Any expenses to claim today?" → Yes → End day, add expenses. (Or later: Me → Extra work & expenses → Expenses → Add expense.)
  2. Enter the amount (₹), a category (Travel, Food, Materials, Other; Prishit edits the list in Settings → Expenses), what it was for and the date. Add another for several.
  3. Attach a receipt photo: optional, but required above ₹500 (Prishit sets the amount in Settings → Expenses).
  4. Submit. It can be withdrawn until Prishit decides.

Claims are accepted for expenses dated in the current month, or in the previous month during the first 5 days of the new one.

Deciding (Prishit)

Approvals → Expenses → Review (the receipt is in the sheet) → Approve, or Reject… with a reason Xyz sees. When he pays it with the salary: People → Xyz → Month → Mark paid (one claim, or Mark all paid, with the date). The team report shows what's approved and not yet paid.

Who sees claims: the person sees their own; Prishit sees everyone's; Prakyat never sees anyone else's, not even his team's.

18Settings Mostly ready

More → Settings. Everything configurable lives here, changeable by Prishit without a developer. Prakyat sees only lists, templates and custom fields for his own clients.

  • Company: name and logo, weekly off days (Sunday), holidays, reminder times, how late a forgotten End day can still be recorded (5:00 AM), acknowledgement and overdue escalation times, workload warning level, email cap.
  • Team: add a person (an Employee by invite, or a Freelancer with a coordinator), role, job title, deactivate and reactivate. Job titles are an editable list (Video Editor, Graphic Designer, …).
  • Lists: expense categories (Settings → Expenses, with the receipt amount), task types (Settings → Task types, Prishit only: the kinds of task, their order and what each asks for), project steps (for example Script → Shoot → Edit → Posted; phase 7). A list still to come shows "Coming soon".
  • Custom fields for clients, contacts, projects, pieces and tasks: for all clients (Prishit only) or for one client (Prakyat for his own). Every client is different, so this is where the app bends to each one. Admins can read client and contact fields, so the form reminds: "Amounts belong in project billing (Owner only)."
  • Templates: task templates (Settings → Templates: a type, a priority, stages and field defaults, shared by everyone; Prakyat edits his own, Prishit any). Project templates come in phase 7.
  • Storage: how much file space MaxOff is using (phase 8).

19Rules that always hold

  1. Exactly three roles: Owner (Prishit), Admin, Crew. Job titles are just labels.
  2. Only Prishit decides attendance and leave, and gives every final approval.
  3. Money is Prishit's only: prices, revenue and reports. The one exception: people see their own expense claims.
  4. Money counts as earned only when Prishit approves the client's piece of work.
  5. Client work and crew tasks are separate. Crew see only their tasks and a client's name on them.
  6. Everyone on a task taps "Task Noted"; only its primary owner taps Mark done.
  7. Nothing is ever deleted or overwritten. Corrections sit next to the original, with who, when and why. Records are closed or archived, not destroyed.
  8. All dates and times are Indian time (IST).
  9. MaxOff collects facts; it never rates people.

20How the app behaves on a phone Ready now

  • It behaves like a real app, not a website. The back gesture closes the top thing first (a list, a dialog, a sheet), then goes back one screen. Switching tabs or months never piles up "back" steps. Back on the home tab closes the app.
  • Screens you open slide in; the ‹ at the top slides them out.
  • Each tab remembers where you scrolled.
  • When you come back to the app, it quietly refreshes the data. The bell and Alerts update while you watch, with no reload (section 14).
  • Tapping a notification opens its screen with the list underneath, so back goes to the list and not out of the app.
  • No pinch-zoom in the installed app, on Android and (from version 1.5) on iPhone; the app follows the phone's own text size instead, up to twice the normal size.
  • First glance, then depth: each tab's first screen shows what needs you now; details are one tap deeper, never more than two.

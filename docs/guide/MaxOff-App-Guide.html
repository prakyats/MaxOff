MaxOff: how the app works

A plain-language guide to the finished app, as decided up to 28 September 2026: who does what, where every screen is, and exactly what happens after each tap. For Pixora Clips. Stage 1 is live at app.maxoff.in.

PrishitOwner. Decides attendance, leave, money and every final approval.
PrakyatAdmin. Runs his assigned clients and checks the work he gives out. Also an employee.
XyzEmployee (Staff). Does the tasks given to them, marks the working day, takes leave.

How to read this guide. Screens are written as tap paths, for example More → Clients → New client. Each section is tagged Ready now (live in the app at https://app.maxoff.in) or Phase N (decided, arriving with that phase). Small details of a screen may change while it is built; the rules in this guide are the decisions.

Stage 1 is live at https://app.maxoff.in: attendance, leave, comp leave, expenses, the month summary, clients and people. Screens from later phases are already in the app and say "coming soon" until they arrive. Next, in this order (staff first; each stage goes live as soon as it is ready): 4 staff tasks · 5 notifications · 6 My Day, Today, calendar, end-of-day report · 8 handing in work · 7 client projects · 9 money and reports · 10 polish and full launch. A tag like Phase 4 means "live once phase 4 is released".

1The app at a glance

MaxOff is Pixora Clips' internal operations system. Clients never log in. It runs on phones and computers, installs like an app, and works in Indian time (IST) everywhere.

Who sees what

Prishit (Owner)Today · Approvals · Tasks · Calendar · More
More holds Reports, Clients, People, Settings and Me.		Nothing is hidden from Prishit. He is exempt from marking attendance.
Prakyat (Admin)Today · Approvals · Tasks · Calendar · More
More holds his Clients, People, his Reports, the parts of Settings he may edit, and Me.		Any money or amount, anyone's attendance or leave records, clients assigned to other Admins, other people's work beyond "busy" blocks, other people's expense claims.
Xyz (Staff)My Day · Tasks · Calendar · Alerts · MeClients' records, projects, money, other people's tasks, approvals, reports, settings.

In stage 1, Tasks, Calendar and Alerts (and Prakyat's Approvals and Reports) are in place and say, in plain words, what they will be for and that they are coming soon. Staff tasks keep running outside MaxOff until phase 4.

The colour rule. The one solid red button on a screen is the one that saves or commits something (Save, Submit, Request leave, the confirming button). A red outline means "careful, this removes or cancels something", and it always asks you to confirm with a button that says exactly what it does ("Deactivate Xyz"), never just "OK". Grey buttons go back or open something.

2Getting in and staying in

Installing the app Ready now

  1. Open https://app.maxoff.in on the phone: in Chrome on Android, in Safari on iPhone. Sign in.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Install app (Android) or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone). On iPhone this step is required for notifications.
  3. MaxOff now opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app. On iPhone, sign in once more inside the app the first time (the installed app keeps its own sign-in).

Joining the team Ready now

  1. Prishit invites a person: More → People → Invite, with email, name, role (Admin or Staff) and job title → Send invite. The invite link appears with Copy, and Prishit sends it on WhatsApp together with the one-page "Your first day with MaxOff". (Invite emails come later; from phase 4 the button is Add person → Employee or Freelancer.)
  2. Xyz opens the link on the phone, sets a password and lands on a welcome screen on Me. The link works once, within 24 hours; if it has expired or was used, Prishit taps People → Xyz → ⋯ → Copy invite link for a fresh one.
  3. Attendance starts the next day: the joining day is never counted.

Staying signed in Ready now

  • Once signed in, people stay signed in, like WhatsApp or Gmail. The app never signs anyone out on its own, so tasks and notifications reach them in the evening and on days off too.
  • Opening the app is not attendance. Attendance is the separate Start day and End day (section 3).
  • Me → Sign out of this device is only for a lost or shared phone. It warns that notifications stop on that device.
  • When Prishit deactivates someone (People → person → ⋯ → Deactivate), they lose access on every device at once. Their history is kept.
  • Forgot password: Sign-in screen → Forgot your password? emails a link (from noreply@mail.maxoff.in, "Set your MaxOff password") that sets a new password.

The Me page Ready now

Shows your name, phone, sign-in email, role and job title as plain text. To change them: Me → ✎ Edit → change a field → Save (red) → a confirmation names the change ("Your name will change from X to Y") → confirm → "Saved". Leaving with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?". Me also holds Attendance & leave, your photo, and Appearance (light, dark or follow the phone).

3Xyz's working day Ready now

Working hours are flexible. MaxOff records when the day really starts and ends, and never guesses.

Morning: Start day

On a working day Xyz opens the app anywhere, any time. Until the day is started, this appears inside the app:

┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ │ Started working? │ │ Start your day to record it. │ │ │ │ [ Start day ] (red) │ │ On leave today? Choose leave │ │ Just looking │ └──────────────────────────────────────┘
  • Start day → the start time is recorded at that tap. The day shows "Present · waiting for approval" until Prishit approves it.
  • On leave today? → choose Leave or Half day, add an optional reason, submit. It goes to Prishit.
  • Just looking → the prompt closes (for example Xyz only wanted to check tasks). It returns when the app is opened or returned to, at most every 30 minutes, until the day is started or leave chosen. My Day also shows a strip: "Not started · Start day".

During the day: My Day Phase 6

● Started 9:12 am · waiting for approval [End day] Needs your "Task Noted" 2 Changes requested 1 Overdue 1 Today 3 Upcoming · Events [ + Request a task ] End day

Everything is one tap away: tapping a line opens those tasks; tapping the status line opens Attendance & leave. In stage 1 My Day is the status line with Start day and End day, and below it "More of your day is coming soon".

Evening: End day

  1. Tap My Day → End day.
  2. Optional: Worked late? Add an overtime note (roughly how long, and what was worked on).
  3. "Any expenses to claim today?" → No (the default), or Yes: the button becomes End day, add expenses and the claim form opens right after (section 17).
  4. Confirm. The end time is recorded. End day is final for that day: it cannot be reopened. The app stays fully usable afterwards (checking tomorrow's tasks at 9 PM is fine and is not work).

Days off (Sundays and holidays)

No prompt and no absence check. If Xyz actually worked, they tap My Day → I worked today and describe the work. Prishit then decides (section 5).

Approved leave days

No prompt. The strip says "On leave today" with I'm working today, which asks to confirm. If Xyz confirms and Prishit approves, the day counts as "1 day worked" and the leave itself stays as it was. On an approved half day the strip says "Half day today" and Start day and End day stay available: the half day stands.

Prakyat (Admin) does exactly the same as Xyz; his Start day and End day live on his Today screen. Prishit never marks attendance.

4Leave Ready now

Asking for leave (Xyz or Prakyat)

  1. My Day → status line or Me → Attendance & leave → Request leave.
  2. Choose Leave (one day or a range), Half day (one date) or Comp leave (only shown when a comp credit is available, section 5). Dates can be today or later, up to a year long. The reason is optional.
  3. Tap Request leave (red). It appears as "Waiting" and in Prishit's Approvals.
  • Withdraw a request while it's still waiting: tap it → Withdraw → confirm.
  • Change or cancel approved leave: tap it → Change / Ask to cancel. This sends a new request to Prishit; the original stays approved until he decides. Possible only until the leave has ended; after that, only Prishit can correct it.
  • Overlapping requests are refused ("request a change instead").
  • The Attendance tab shows each day of the month with its full story in plain words ("You chose Present", "Prishit changed it to Absent: …" with his reason).
Only Prishit decides leave. Prakyat has no say in anyone's attendance or leave, including his own.

5Extra work and comp leave Ready now

Recording extra work (Xyz)

  • Overtime note: Attendance & leave → Extra work → Add note (or at End day). Pick the day (up to 7 days back), roughly how long (optional) and what was worked on (required).
  • Worked on a day off: My Day → I worked today on a Sunday or holiday (or later, up to 7 days back), with a note about the work.

Prishit reviews it

Approvals → Extra work → Review → Decide…. For each note: Grant 1 day, Grant ½ day or No comp leave. For day-off work he also decides whether to mark the day as worked. Nothing is automatic: if the extra work came from Xyz's own mistake, for example, Prishit simply grants nothing. Xyz sees the outcome on the note: "1 comp leave granted · use by 31 Oct" or "Reviewed by the Owner".

Prishit can also grant comp leave at any time, for any reason, without a note: People → Xyz → Leave → Grant comp leave (½ or 1 day, optional note Xyz sees). A double tap grants it once. He can take back an unused grant with Revoke, with a reason Xyz reads.

Using comp leave (Xyz)

  1. Xyz sees the balance on Attendance & leave → Extra work: "1½ days of comp leave · use by 31 Oct".
  2. Request leave → Comp leave (1 day) or Comp leave, half day (½) → pick the date from the list. The list holds only working days (no Sundays, no holidays) up to the end of the month the credit was granted in.
  3. It goes to Prishit like any leave. He can refuse it (for example, there's a shoot that day). While it waits, the credit is held; if refused, withdrawn or cancelled, the credit comes back (unless its month has ended).
Credits expire at the end of the calendar month they were granted in, even if that's two days later. Expired credits stay visible in the history. If Prishit later adds a holiday on a date that already has comp leave (waiting or approved), that comp leave is cancelled and the credit goes back to Xyz, with a note saying which holiday.

6Prishit's attendance tools

Today Ready now (full screen in phase 6)

Today's attendance › 11 Present · 2 On leave · 1 Waiting · 1 Not started Needs you Xyz ● Waiting for decision › Abc ● Not started yet › [ See all 15 people ]

Tapping a count opens the full list filtered to that group ("Waiting" opens Approvals). Tapping a person opens their page.

Approving attendance and leave Ready now

Approvals is one screen, grouped: Attendance · Leave · Extra work · Expenses (and, when they arrive, Staff tasks · Client items). The badge on the tab shows how many things wait. Extra work and Expenses are review-only: every one is opened and decided on its own.

  • Approve one row → it fades, with a 6-second Undo. Nothing is saved until the Undo time passes (or the app is left).
  • Approve all N on a group → a confirmation with the count → "Approve N" (red). Only the rows on screen are approved.
  • Review → a sheet with the details → Correct the day (choose the right status and write a reason) or Reject leave (with a reason). The form says "Xyz will see this reason".
  • Correcting a leave day to Present counts it as "1 day worked"; the approved leave stays, and a note says so.
  • Approving leave that covers days Prishit has already decided leaves those days as he decided, and says so ("These days keep your earlier decision").

A person's page Ready now

Today → tap a person or More → People → tap a person. Tabs: Profile (details, Edit, and ⋯ for Deactivate, Reactivate, Copy invite link), Leave (their requests, with Edit and Cancel on approved leave), Attendance (month by month, with Correct on every day). The ‹ at the top goes back.

The month summary Ready now

People → person → Month, and the team report at More → Reports → Month, live at any time (section 16).

7Clients Ready now

Adding a client (Prishit)

  1. More → Clients → New client: name (required, unique), and anything else known: business name, address, GSTIN, phone, email, website, requirements.
  2. The client opens in Draft. Add contacts (one is marked primary), the Brand kit (logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice, notes), the Google Drive folder link and any custom fields.
  3. ⋯ → Assign Admin → Prakyat. Prakyat is notified: "You now run Sharma Weddings" (once notifications arrive, phase 5).
  4. ⋯ → Activate. The client is live.

The client page

Tabs: Overview (details, contacts, custom fields) · Brand · Projects (phase 7) · Staff tasks labelled with this client (phase 4) · Activity (everything that changed). Prishit also sees his private notes and, later, money panels.

  • Pause: everything stays visible and editable, but monthly work stops creating new rounds. Close (Inactive): stays searchable, no new work until Prishit reactivates it. Only Prishit changes the state.
  • Prakyat sees and edits only the clients assigned to him. Xyz never sees a client's page, only the client's name and brand basics on a task.
  • The client list: cards with logo, name, state and Admin; search and filters; opens on Active clients.
  • Brand colours are swatches: each colour shows as a chip with its name and hex code, and a tap copies the code ("Copied #E11D48"). Fonts are a simple list, each with an optional note on where it's used. In Brand → Edit each colour is a row with a colour picker, a name and the hex code; rows can be added, moved up or down and removed, and Save names every change.
  • Closing a client asks for an optional reason. Only Prishit ever sees it, on the client's Activity.
  • No client is ever left without an Admin. If Prishit makes Prakyat Staff, or deactivates him, the same confirmation first asks "Move Prakyat's 3 clients to: [Admin]" (one Admin for all, or Choose per client), then makes the change.

8Client projects and the monthly round Phase 7

Every client's work is organised as projects, and each project is made of pieces of work (items), for example "Reel 1 – Ambience".

Sharma Weddings ├── Monthly Production every month · Retainer │ └── October: Reel 1 – Ambience · Reel 2 – Menu · … · Post 1 · Post 2 ├── Drone Documentation every week · Retainer │ └── Week 1 · Week 2 · … └── Brand Film one-time · Additional Work └── Make the video

Creating a project (Prakyat or Prishit)

  1. Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → New project (or start from a saved template).
  2. Name it, choose how it repeats (monthly, weekly or one-time), optionally pick the steps every piece goes through (for example Script → Shoot → Edit → Posted), and list the pieces.
  3. Prishit only sets the money: an amount per month (split across the pieces, or a value per piece) or a fixed amount for a one-time project, and the category (Retainer, Project or Additional Work). Prakyat never sees any of it.

The monthly round

  • On the 1st (or every Monday for weekly work), MaxOff creates the new round for every active client, copying the list of pieces. Prakyat renames them for the month's themes.
  • The project page shows the round (switch between months), each piece with its steps, and progress like "9/12 done · 8/12 approved".
  • Prakyat ticks steps as the work happens and marks a piece done when it's delivered. Several pieces can be ticked at once.
Planned: a place on Prakyat's and Prishit's Today that shows every client's pieces due this week and overdue, so daily work isn't four screens deep. Settled at the start of phase 7.

9Staff tasks, from creation to final approval Phase 4

Tasks are how work is given to people. They are separate from client projects: Xyz sees tasks, never projects.

Creating a task (Prakyat or Prishit)

  1. Tasks → New task (or from a task template).
  2. Fill in: title, description, type (Normal, Shoot / Site Visit, Meeting, Posting, Review, Other…), optional client label (Sharma Weddings), priority, deadline (date and time), and the people: Xyz as the main person, others as helpers.
  3. MaxOff warns (never blocks) if Xyz is on approved leave that day, has overlapping timed work, or already has a heavy day.
  4. Create task (red). Xyz is notified at once.

Shoots, site visits, meetings and postings also get an event date, time, place and purpose, and appear on the Calendar.

Xyz receives it

  1. The task appears in My Day → Needs your "Task Noted".
  2. Xyz taps Task Noted, which records that it was seen, and when. Every person on the task must do this.
  3. If nobody taps it: reminders every 2 hours, then after 4 hours Prakyat is told, and after 8 hours Prishit is told. (All times are settings.)

Doing and finishing the work

  • Xyz adds comments and updates, ticks steps, and hands in files or links (section 10).
  • Only the main person taps Done. If the deadline has passed, a reason is required.
  • After Done, the task is locked for the assignees (comments still work) until someone asks for changes.
  • A task is overdue the moment its deadline passes without final approval. If nothing was handed in 24 hours after the deadline, Prakyat and Prishit are told.

  2. Changes requested: the task goes back to Xyz as "Changes requested" (top of My Day). Xyz fixes it and hands in version 2. Every version is kept.
  3. Prishit gives the final approval: Approvals → Staff tasks → Approve (or several at once). Only now is the task complete.

  • Suggesting a task: Xyz can tap My Day → Request a task. Prakyat or Prishit turns it into a real task or declines it with a reason.

10Handing in work (files and links) Phase 8

  1. On the task: Hand in work.
  2. Photos and documents (up to 25 MB) and short videos (up to 100 MB) upload straight from the phone or computer. Photos are kept exactly as taken, full quality, including iPhone formats; MaxOff makes a small preview so they open anywhere.
  3. Large videos: paste a Google Drive link to the file. If a file is too big to upload, MaxOff says so and asks for a link.
  4. Each hand-in is a version (v1, v2 …). Prakyat and Prishit preview it, download the original, comment, and approve or ask for changes on that exact version.
For the first version, a pasted Drive link points to Xyz's own file: if they delete or unshare it, it's gone. The automatic company copy in Pixora's Google Drive comes after the launch (section 21).

11Turning finished work into earned money Phases 7 and 9

A task being approved does not tick the client's piece of work by itself. The two are confirmed separately, on purpose:

  1. Xyz's task "Edit Reel 1" is approved (section 9).
  2. Prakyat opens Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → Monthly Production, ticks the steps (Edit ✓, Posted ✓) and marks Reel 1 done.
  3. Prishit sees it in Approvals → Client items and taps Approve (one or several), or sends it back to Prakyat with a reason.
  4. Only after Prishit's approval does Reel 1's value count as earned. Until then it is only "planned".

Prishit's money view (Reports, and panels on each client) shows for each client, category and month: Potential (planned), Achieved (approved) and Remaining. He can adjust a figure with a note; the original calculation is always kept beside it.

12Additional work Phase 7

The client asks for something outside the monthly package, for example one extra reel:

  1. Clients → Sharma Weddings → Projects → New project: "Extra Reel – Engagement", one-time.
  2. Prishit sets its category to Additional Work and gives it a price.
  3. From here it follows the same path: a task for Xyz, hand-in, Prakyat's check, Prishit's approval, Prakyat marks the piece done, Prishit approves it, and it counts as earned.

Keeping it as its own project means the monthly package's numbers stay clean, and Additional Work shows separately in the reports.

13Freelancers Phase 4

  • Freelancers don't get a login. Prishit adds one with People → Add person → Freelancer: name, job title, optional phone, and a coordinator (an employee, for example Xyz).
  • Tasks can be given to the freelancer like anyone else. Xyz, as coordinator, does everything for them from Xyz's own account: Task Noted, hand-ins, Done. The record says so: "Done by Xyz for the freelancer".
  • Notifications meant for the freelancer go to Xyz.
  • No attendance, leave or expense claims for freelancers. Their work shows separately in reports. Payments to freelancers are not in the first version.

14Notifications Phase 5

  • Notifications are mandatory and go only to the people concerned: in the app (Alerts, or the bell) and as phone notifications. Tapping one opens the right screen.
  • Because people stay signed in, notifications show the full detail on the lock screen, for example "Reel 4 for Sharma Weddings is due tomorrow 10 AM". Amounts never appear in a notification, not even Prishit's.
  • Email is used only for invites, task assigned, escalations, an event tomorrow (shoot, meeting), and Prishit's daily digest, with a daily cap per person.
  • Task reminders by default: 2 days before, 1 day before, at the deadline, and when overdue.
  • Me → Send a test confirms a phone really receives notifications. Prishit sees who isn't reachable (notifications blocked, iPhone app not installed, repeated failures) so no one misses a shoot silently.

16End of the month

Unfinished client work Phase 7

MaxOff highlights every piece not finished in the month's round. For each one Prishit chooses:

  • Carry forward: it moves into next month but keeps its original month and value, so next month's package isn't inflated.
  • Close (with a reason): kept, never deleted; its value stays visible as "not achieved".
  • Leave pending.

Billing and closing the month Phase 9

  • Prishit marks each client's month Billed (optional date and note). MaxOff doesn't send invoices.
  • Reports → Close month saves a permanent record of the month. Later mistakes are fixed as dated corrections, never by overwriting.

17Expense claims Ready now

Claiming (Xyz or Prakyat)

  1. At End day: "Any expenses to claim today?" → Yes → End day, add expenses. (Or later: Attendance & leave → Expenses → Add expense.)
  2. Enter the amount (₹), a category (Travel, Food, Materials, Other; Prishit edits the list in Settings → Expenses), what it was for and the date. Add another for several.
  3. Attach a receipt photo: optional, but required above ₹500 (Prishit sets the amount in Settings → Expenses).
  4. Submit. It can be withdrawn until Prishit decides.

Claims are accepted for expenses dated in the current month, or in the previous month during the first 5 days of the new one.

Deciding (Prishit)

Approvals → Expenses → Review (the receipt is in the sheet) → Approve, or Reject… with a reason Xyz sees. When he pays it with the salary: People → Xyz → Month → Mark paid (one claim, or Mark all paid, with the date). The team report shows what's approved and not yet paid.

Who sees claims: the person sees their own; Prishit sees everyone's; Prakyat never sees anyone else's, not even his team's.

18Settings Mostly ready

More → Settings. Everything configurable lives here, changeable by Prishit without a developer. Prakyat sees only lists, templates and custom fields for his own clients.

  • Company: name and logo, weekly off days (Sunday), holidays, reminder times, how late a forgotten End day can still be recorded (5:00 AM), acknowledgement and overdue escalation times, workload warning level, email cap.
  • Team: invite a person (and, from phase 4, add a Freelancer with a coordinator), role, job title, deactivate and reactivate. Job titles are an editable list (Video Editor, Graphic Designer, …).
  • Lists: expense categories (Settings → Expenses, with the receipt amount), task types (phase 4), project steps (for example Script → Shoot → Edit → Posted; phase 7). A list still to come shows "Coming soon".
  • Custom fields for clients, contacts, projects, pieces and tasks: for all clients (Prishit only) or for one client (Prakyat for his own). Every client is different, so this is where the app bends to each one. Admins can read client and contact fields, so the form reminds: "Amounts belong in project billing (Owner only)."
  • Templates: project templates and task templates (phases 4 and 7).
  • Storage: how much file space MaxOff is using (phase 8).

19Rules that always hold

  1. Exactly three roles: Owner (Prishit), Admin, Staff. Job titles are just labels.
  2. Only Prishit decides attendance and leave, and gives every final approval.
  3. Money is Prishit's only: prices, revenue and reports. The one exception: people see their own expense claims.
  4. Money counts as earned only when Prishit approves the client's piece of work.
  5. Client work and staff tasks are separate. Staff see only their tasks and a client's name on them.
  6. Everyone on a task taps "Task Noted"; only the main person taps Done.
  7. Nothing is ever deleted or overwritten. Corrections sit next to the original, with who, when and why. Records are closed or archived, not destroyed.
  8. All dates and times are Indian time (IST).
  9. MaxOff collects facts; it never rates people.

20How the app behaves on a phone Ready now

  • It behaves like a real app, not a website. The back gesture closes the top thing first (a list, a dialog, a sheet), then goes back one screen. Switching tabs or months never piles up "back" steps. Back on the home tab closes the app.
  • Screens you open slide in; the ‹ at the top slides them out.
  • Each tab remembers where you scrolled.
  • When you come back to the app, it quietly refreshes the data. Live updates while you watch come with notifications in phase 5.
  • No pinch-zoom on Android; the app follows the phone's own text size instead.
  • First glance, then depth: each tab's first screen shows what needs you now; details are one tap deeper, never more than two.

Your first day with MaxOff

Pixora Clips' app for your working day, your tasks, leave and expenses. Keep this on your phone.

MaxOff records when your day starts and ends, the tasks you are given, your leave and your expense claims. Prishit (the Owner) approves them. Working hours are flexible: MaxOff records what really happens and never guesses. All times are Indian time.

1Get the app

  1. Open the invite link Prishit sent you and tap Continue to MaxOff. It works once, within 24 hours. Choose a password (at least 12 characters) → Save password and sign in.
  2. Android: open https://app.maxoff.in in Chrome → ⋮ → Install app.
    iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, then open MaxOff from the home screen and sign in once more.
  3. From now on, open MaxOff from its icon. You stay signed in.
Your attendance starts tomorrow: the day you join is never counted. Calendar and Alerts say "coming soon" for now.

2Turn on notifications

This is how a new task, a decision on your leave or a change request reaches you. Do it once, on your phone.

  1. iPhone: do Add to Home Screen first (step 1) and open MaxOff from its icon. An iPhone notifies only the installed app.
  2. MaxOff shows a band, "Turn on notifications". Tap Turn on.
  3. Your phone asks for permission: tap Allow. The band goes away.
  4. Check it: Me → Send a test notification → Send test.
  • Quiet hours: between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM nothing is sent to your phone. At 7:00 AM you get one notification with what happened overnight ("3 updates while you were away").
  • A new task is also emailed to you.
  • Tapping a notification opens what it is about.

3Every working day

Start. Open MaxOff when you start work. It asks:

Started working? Start your day to record it. [ Start day ] On leave today? Choose leave Just looking
  • Start day: the start time is the moment you tap. Your day shows "waiting for approval" until Prishit approves it.
  • Just looking: only checking something? It closes, and asks again later until you start.

End. When you finish: My Day → End day.

  • Worked late? Add an overtime note: how long, and what you worked on.
  • "Any expenses to claim today?" No, or Yes to claim them straight away (section 6).
  • Confirm. End day is final for that day. You can still use the app afterwards.
  • Forgot to end it? MaxOff reminds you in the evening. Until 5:00 AM the next morning (and before you start the new day), My Day offers Yesterday not ended · End day. After that the day stays "End of day not recorded". No time is ever made up: if you worked late, add an overtime note.
  • Your days off (Sundays to start with) and holidays: no question at all. Worked anyway? My Day → I worked today.

Admins: the same, on Today.

4Your tasks

Work is given to you as tasks, on the Tasks tab. My tasks is always in this order: Not noted · Changes requested · Overdue · Due today · Upcoming. Open a task and it shows one button: your next step.

  1. Noted. A new task is under Not noted. Open it and tap Task Noted: it says you have seen it. Everyone on the task does this.
  2. Start. Tap Start work when you begin.
  3. Done. When it is finished, tap Mark done. In the note, paste the Google Drive link to your work (share the file or folder first, so the reviewer can open it). Files can't be uploaded in MaxOff yet. Past the deadline, it asks why it's late.
  • Only the task's primary owner marks it done. Details on the task shows who that is.
  • After Mark done the task is locked while it is checked. Prishit gives the final approval; an Admin may check it first.
  • Changes requested? The task comes back with the reason at the top. Fix it, then Mark done again with the new link.
  • Chat on the task is for questions and updates. "Chat · 2 new", or a small speech bubble on a task in the list, means comments you haven't read.
  • Work has the brief and the stages to tick; Activity has everything that happened.
  • See something that needs doing? Tasks → Suggest a task. Prishit or an Admin makes it a task, or tells you why not.

5Leave

  • Me → Attendance & leave → Request leave → Leave (a day or a range) or Half day (one date) → a reason if you like → Request leave.
  • On leave today? Answer the morning question with On leave today? Choose leave.
  • Prishit approves or rejects every request. Withdraw it while it waits; to change or cancel approved leave, tap it and ask. The original stays until he decides.
  • On a half day, Start day and End day still work as usual.
  • Comp leave shows in the form only when Prishit has given you comp leave, usually for extra work.

6Expenses

  • At End day: "Any expenses to claim today?" → Yes → End day, add expenses. Or any time: Me → Attendance & leave → Expenses → Add expense.
  • Enter the amount (₹), a category, what it was for and the date. Add another for more.
  • A receipt photo is needed above ₹500 (Prishit sets the amount). Claims are for this month, or last month during the first 5 days of a new one.
  • You can withdraw a claim until Prishit decides. You'll see Approved, Rejected (with his reason) or Paid.

7Who sees what

WhoWhat they see of you
Prishit (Owner)Everything: your start and end times, leave, notes, expense claims and tasks. He decides each one, and when he changes something you see his reason in Attendance & leave.
AdminsThe tasks they give you or check, and those for their clients. Never your times, notes, reasons or claims: when an Admin gives you a task, MaxOff only warns them if you are on leave or already busy that day.
Other CrewOnly the tasks you share with them.
YouYour own tasks, attendance, leave and claims. On a task, the client's name; never a client's page, money or other people's tasks.

MaxOff collects facts; it never rates you, and nothing is overwritten.

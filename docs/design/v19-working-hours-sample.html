Working hours: the v1.9 screens

These screens are new (they don't exist today), so there's no "before". They use the v1.8 look so v1.9 adds features without a new style. Attendance stays Owner-only: Admins see only their own day, like the Crew.

1 · Crew and Admins, their own day

Starting late, with the reason pre-filled

  • Tapping Start day after 10:15 opens a short sheet: how late, and one line why. If they have a shoot later that day, "Shoot at 2:00 pm" is already filled in; they can confirm or edit. It never blocks.
  • My Day then shows one quiet strip: started time, Late chip, and when the day ends.

Leaving early: ask, then wait

  • Before the day's end time, End day becomes Ask to leave early: a reason, and a clear promise of what happens next.
  • While waiting, a calm status shows the minutes; after 30 minutes with no answer, End day comes back and the day is marked "Left early, not answered".

3 · Owner only

Deciding, and "Stay until"

  • Early-leave requests arrive in Approvals with Stay (outline) and Approve (green). "Left early, not answered" days get Approve or Correct.
  • Today shows a Late count (amber, hidden at zero). "Stay until" is in a person's ⋯ on the people board: pick a time after 6:00 pm; they're told at once.

  • Choosing a half day asks which half, as two clear cards. A morning half is never "Late"; an afternoon half is never "leaving early".

Task chat, as a real chat

The task page keeps its header; the Chat tab becomes a dedicated conversation panel with its own scroll and a message box that stays at the bottom. Messages from the same person group together, bubbles fit their text, and days are divided.

Show the "after" mocks in
What changes

From a list of rows to a conversation

  • Its own panel: the chat fills the space under the tabs and scrolls inside itself; the task title, status and tabs never scroll away. The message box is pinned at the bottom.
  • Grouping: consecutive messages from one person within 5 minutes share one name and photo; time shows once at the end of the group.
  • Bubbles fit the text: "ok" is a small bubble, a long message wraps at about 75 % width. Yours sit on the right in the solid neutral colour; others on the left in a soft surface.
  • Day dividers (Today, Yesterday, Mon 20 Oct) and a red "New" line where unread messages start; it opens at the first unread, or the latest.
  • "Jump to latest" appears when you've scrolled up and a message arrives.
  • Small task events (noted, deadline moved, reminder sent) show as a quiet centred line, so the chat tells the story; full detail stays in History.
  • Message box: rounded, grows with the text up to 5 lines; Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a new line (laptop); a round send button. On phone, the chat opens full height and the box sits above the keyboard.
  • Fast: loads the latest 50 messages, "Load earlier" at the top; new messages appear without reloading.

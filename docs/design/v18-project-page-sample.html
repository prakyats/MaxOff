Long stage lists: how v1.8 fixes the real case

Shaped like the team's real project: two deliverables with 12 long-named stages each. Today they show as two screens of centred, wrapping chips. In v1.8 each is one line until you open it, and opening it shows a tidy checklist across the full width.

AOne line until opened

Name, progress bar, "5/12" and the next step. A long next step ends in "…" (full text on hover or tap) instead of wrapping to three lines.

BA checklist, not chips

Tick box plus name, left-aligned, at most two lines, in three columns across the full width. The next stage is bold and outlined.

CA real progress bar

One continuous bar instead of 12 tiny dots, so progress reads at a glance.

DGroup stages, when they choose

Long single lists offer "Group stages". The Admin picks where each group starts. Existing data never changes on its own.

ERounds the easy way

A tip points to "+ Another revision round", so nobody has to type Round 1, Round 2 stages.

FOn the phone

The same one-line rows; opening one shows the checklist in a single column, with the same long-name rule.

What changed from today (refined 16 Oct: full-width month tabs and Details, History and ⋯ in the header, ⋯ on every row, as the live app has)

01Red only means "look here"

"Mark done" is no longer red on every row. It's a dark button that only appears when a deliverable is ready. Red is kept for "1 day late".

02A "needs you" summary

Next to the progress ring: what's late and what's nearly done, so you see the problem before scrolling.

03Groups as progress strips

Each stage group is one tidy line with its own bar and count, instead of a wall of chips.

04"Next" tells you what to do

One tap ticks the next stage. Everything else opens with the › on the row.

05Calmer surfaces

Cards sit on the page with a soft tone step and one gentle shadow, instead of heavy borders. Consistent corners everywhere.

06Cleaner type

A clear size ladder: a bold title, a quieter subtitle, readable small text. Nothing tiny, and numbers line up.

07Aligned columns on laptop

Deliverable, progress, next step and date line up, so you can scan down a column.

08Months as tabs

Switching months is a quick tab, not a big box.

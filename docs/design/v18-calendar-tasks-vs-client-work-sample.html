Calendar: tasks vs client work

Today task chips and client deliverables look the same in the calendar. This sample makes them different at a glance, adds a quick All · Tasks · Client work switch for the Owner and Admins, and splits the day popup into two sections. The Crew never see client work, so their calendar doesn't change.

Show the "after" mocks in
Month view

Two kinds of items, two looks

  • Tasks (shoots, meetings, due dates): the filled soft chip with the task-type dot, as approved.
  • Client deliverables: an outlined chip with a small box icon and the client name first ("Shetty · Reel 1"); a late one gets a red outline.
  • Same order in every box: timed tasks, then due tasks, then client work, then "+N more".
  • A switch next to Filters: All · Tasks · Client work (Owner and Admins; remembers your choice).

1 · Owner & Admin (the task's creator)

Putting a task on hold, and resuming it new

  • Hold is in the task's ⋯. A short sheet asks why (required), and the assignees get a quiet notice.
  • While on hold the task shows an amber On hold chip and a line with the reason. It leaves to-do and overdue lists, and no reminders or escalations go out.
  • Resume is the one button on the hold line. If the deadline has passed, it asks for a new deadline (required); the old one stays in History.

2 · Owner & the client's Admin

Putting a project on hold, and resuming it new

  • Put on hold… is in the project's ⋯, with a reason. No new monthly or weekly cycles are made and no escalations go out. Its deliverables show On hold.
  • At month end: held deliverables stay in their own month, marked On hold. There's no automatic carry-forward and no "decide these" nag while on hold. (Advisor's recommendation: please confirm.)
  • Resume lists every deliverable whose planned date has passed, to give each a new date in one go. Any whose month has ended also gets the usual Carry forward / Close / Leave pending choice.
  • Reports (Owner only): held deliverables are their own "On hold" line, neither earned nor lost.

Clear Late: sample for the owner (v7.0)

What you chose on 14 Nov (Q2): when someone is Late only because their signal was down, their day shows "Tried at 9:58, no network (phone time)", and Approvals gives you a one-tap Clear Late. Nothing is cleared by itself.

  • The server's time stays the record. The phone's time is only a note beside it, because a phone clock can be changed.
  • Clear Late is your correction, with a reason written for you ("No network at the start; tried 9:58 by the phone's clock"). The Late minutes stay in the history, marked cleared. They no longer count in the month's Late days.
  • Only you see Clear Late. Admins get no attendance controls, as today.
  • The row appears only when the day was Late and the phone recorded a try before the start time. Every other Late day is unchanged.

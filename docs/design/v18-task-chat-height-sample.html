Task chat: same width, much taller

The chat keeps its place and its full width under the Work | Chat tabs. The tabs keep today's look and position on both tabs. The chat gains height in three ways: a tighter header above it, a panel that always reaches the bottom of the screen, and tabs that stay at the top as you scroll, so the chat can fill almost the whole screen. ⤢ still enlarges it further.

1 · Laptop, first view

A tighter header, the same on Work and Chat

  • Nothing is removed or hidden: status, priority, due/overdue, next step, Remind, People and the approval route all stay in the first view.
  • Priority and due share one line (with the red overdue), and Remind sits inside the next-step box on its right, instead of on a row of its own. The header is about 150 px shorter.
  • The tabs sit higher, but in the same place on Work and on Chat, so switching never moves them.
  • The chat runs from the tabs to the bottom of the screen, and the message box is pinned there. On your laptop that's about 8–10 messages in the first view, up from about 5.

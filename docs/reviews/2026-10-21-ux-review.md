## Bugs: fix first in v1.8 (unit V0)

- **B1 Times in three formats.** Today `started 21:51` vs Approvals `started 9:51 pm`; Alerts title `11:59 pm`, body `due 19 Oct, 18:00`; End of day `saves at 5:00 AM` above `Saved 20 Oct, 5:00 am`; Tasks `6:00 PM`. Fix: one formatter in `core/time` used by every screen, notification body and email: `6:00 pm` (lower-case, 12-hour).
- **B2 "Needs you" counts don't match the rows.** Owner Tasks `Needs you · 2` shows 3 rows (the "1 task waiting for your approval" link + 2 tasks); Admin Today `Needs you · 2` shows 3 rows. Fix: the count equals the rows shown.
- **B3 A task awaiting the Owner sits under "Open tasks".** Owner Tasks: `Test 6 oct · Admin approved · needs you` is listed under Open tasks. Fix: anything labelled "needs you" lives in Needs you.
- **B4 The attendance Review panel has no Approve.** Approvals → Review on an attendance row: a side panel with Proposed, Started and only `Correct…`. Fix: Approve (primary) and Correct in the panel, plus context (the person's week, leave balance, any note). Same transition functions as the row; no new permission.
- **B5 A reopened task shows the old "Marked done" hand-in.** Task "Edit" is In progress, Hand-in reads `Marked done by PShetty, 9 Oct · Late: Idk`; nothing says it was reopened (16 Oct, "simple"). Owner and Crew both. Fix: `Earlier hand-in · reopened 16 Oct: "simple"`, collapsed under the current state.
- **B6 Activity mentions a question Chat doesn't show.** Same task: Activity `Prishit Shetty asked PShetty about the task`; Chat `No comments yet`. Fix: the question appears in Chat (or Activity links to where it went). Find what "asked about" wrote and where.
- **B7 Revision rounds land after the last stage.** "+ Another revision round" on `Shoot → Edit → Posted → Script` appended `Revision round 1`, `Revision round 2` after Script/Posted. **Owner decision 2026-10-21 (option a):** the new round goes right after the last existing revision stage, or right after the "Edit" stage when there is none; never at the end after "Posted". If neither exists, ask before guessing (record it in PROGRESS).
- **B8 A cancelled project says "No deliverables in this cycle".** After cancelling, the ring reads `0% · No deliverables in this cycle` with "Done and closed (1)" below. Fix: `Cancelled 21 Oct · 1 deliverable closed`.
- **B9 Admin sees 3 clients on Today, 2 on Clients.** Today `My clients · 3` includes a non-Active client; Clients opens filtered to Active. Fix: show the state beside the name on Today (or count Active only and say so).
- **B10 "overdue by 33 h".** Past 24 h say days: `overdue 1 day`.
- **B11 Version on Me reads `main · 36b4b95b`.** The guide tells people to quote it. Fix: production shows `v1.7.1`; staging `staging · 36b4b95`.
- **B12 Broken symbol in New project.** `open a deliverable (⌄) to edit its stages` renders as a dot (`src/core/ui/stage-groups/deliverables-editor.tsx:433`). Fix: words, `open a deliverable's row to edit its stages`.
- **B13 One status, two colours.** `Absent (proposed)` has a red dot in End of day, amber elsewhere. Fix: one shared status → colour map.

## v1.8 design items

**Long stage lists (approved 17 Oct, sample v3):** confirmed on staging with a 12-stage, long-named deliverable: on laptop the stages stack as centred chips in a ~170 px column and "Next" wraps to four lines; on phone the row is already fine. The approved A–G fixes stand; laptop is where they matter.

Layout
- **L1** Every page has its own width (Today/Tasks/Reports ~620 px; Clients/People/Calendar full; client Overview ~470 px; the project page mixes). One page grid: lists and boards full width, reading text ≤ ~70 characters.
- **L2** Tables scroll inside a box (People, a person's Attendance): let the page scroll, sticky header.
- **L3** Floating buttons cover content on phone (People "Add person" over a status, Templates "Add template" over Preview): bottom padding under lists.
- **L4** Two header bars on phone (logo bar + title bar, ~90 px): one bar.
- **L5** The project ⋯ menu on phone is a narrow dropdown with wrapped items: bottom sheet.
- **L6** The client page opens on contacts and ten "Not added" fields: Overview leads with active projects and progress; empty fields fold into "Add details".
- **L7** Calendar on laptop has no day list (phone has); month cells say `1 overdue` without naming it; leave bars faint: a selected-day panel, names in cells.
- **L8** Five near-identical "PShetty · Absent (proposed)" rows in Needs you: group per person (`PShetty · 5 days proposed absent · Review`).
- **L9** New task has 11 fields before Create; New project has two help paragraphs per section and a "Search and preview templates" that looks like text: essentials first, the rest under "More options"; a real button.

Colour rule (ADR option A, 6b.8)
- **C1** Save/Create are red (Save company, Save days off, Save thresholds, Save receipt amount, Create task, Create project, Job titles "Add"): neutral primary.
- **C2** Approvals: a solid Approve on every row plus solid Approve all: quiet row Approve, one solid per layer.
- **C3** Red archive icons on every Settings row (Expenses, Job titles, Task types, Custom fields) but grey on Templates; three red "Remove" on Me: grey, red only in the confirm step.
- **C4** Three "Add" styles (outline, solid red, solid white top-right): one pattern.
- **C5** "Cancelled" chips in red: neutral.

Words
- **W1** "items" and "deliverables" both shown (Calendar phone "Client items", Reports, Alerts "Client items: 7 done", Thresholds "Client item overdue", Custom fields tab "Items", Templates "an item list"): "deliverables" wherever people read.
- **W2** The bell opens "Alerts" but the guide says notifications; the menu says "Profile" but the page is "Me": Alerts and Me everywhere.
- **W3** "Cancel" beside "Cancel project": `Keep project` / `Cancel project`.
- **W4** Wrong-audience lines: the Owner's Me says "(the Owner changes this)"; Month report "No salary: you work out pay from these."; End of day "Never money."; Owner viewing a person's leave sees "Your reason: …" for his own cancel.
- **W5** Said twice: chip `Overdue` + line `Past its deadline`; People role column + the role again under the name.
- **W6** Numbers that need decoding: `Leave · Half days · Absent 0 · 0 · 10`; `median 20.2 days`.

Roles and navigation
- **N1** The Admin's own Attendance & leave and Extra work & expenses are only under Me (the Crew have them in the sidebar): add them to the Admin's sidebar and More.
- **N2** The guide is only in Settings → Help and Me: "Help" in the profile menu and the sidebar foot, every role.
- **N3** Settings on phone show 12 bare names (laptop has descriptions): the v1.8 Settings groups with one line each.
- **N4** The Admin's Clients list has an Admin column (always themselves): open deliverables and progress instead.
- **N5** Admin Today offers `Mark done` at `0/6`: "Next" until ready, then a neutral Mark done (as in the sample).

Polish
- **P1** Custom fields render smaller and fainter than built-in fields; empty shows "—" vs "Not added": one style, one empty wording.
- **P2** Small targets: the ↑ ↓ reorder arrows in Settings lists, the weekly-off checkboxes: 44 px.
- **P3** The undo toast covers the page header: bottom toast.
- **P4** Two progress-bar designs side by side on a client's Projects tab: one continuous bar.
- **P5** Thresholds: Save at the bottom of three screens: the 6b.6 groups plus a sticky Save.
